## Current Cycle: Playtest & Hand-Off Readiness (established 2026-07-08)

**Goal:** a stranger can be handed the book, read it cold, run the Integration Tutorial, and continue into a campaign. Assessment (2026-07-08): the system is mechanically cohesive after the note-review cycle; the book is ready for a Levels 1–9 playtest once Tier 1 lands. The two structural boundaries are the Level 10 class wall (class generation is fully delegated to the System AI with no framework behind it; a campaign hits this around session 6) and the System AI being invoked throughout but explained nowhere. The book's deeper hand-off problem: it is a GM reference that assumes you already know the game — it opens with a reference card, buries the premise in the Principles ontology and the tutorial, and never shows what play sounds like.

### The Three Ways to Run (decision in progress)

Context, captured 2026-07-08. Gabriel's long-standing plan is a **companion app**: an AI listening app at the table that transcribes play and writes HVE log entries (axis, intensity, context) automatically — the app *is* the Hidden Vector Engine's bookkeeper, and could also run the System AI generation functions (classes, Personal Opportunities, visions). Whether it exists before the first playtest is ~50/50. He has been hedging: wanting the game runnable without the app but not committing to that in the text. The three modes to document in the expanded System AI chapter:

- **Mode A — Companion App:** live listening → automatic HVE log; GM reviews and curates entries; app runs generation functions on demand. (Needs a player-consent note: the table is being recorded.)
- **Mode B — AI-Assisted:** no bespoke app; the GM keeps the HVE log manually (the tutorial's Vector Logging Cheatsheet already does this) and pastes it into any LLM chat with the book's prompt templates for class generation, Personal Opportunities, Battle Memory visions, and Compression refinement. Between-sessions work, not live.
- **Mode C — Unplugged:** no AI at all. Requires the **Paper HVE** (Tier 1 item below) and a static class catalog (Tier 2, part of the class framework).

**DECIDED (2026-07-08): Mode C is the floor — the book must be fully runnable unplugged — and Modes A/B are amplifiers.** Recorded as a standing commitment ("The Unplugged Floor") in CLAUDE.md. Rationale: the hand-off goal fails if the book requires software that is 50/50 to exist; a print RPG shouldn't be hostage to an app's lifecycle; and specifying the manual procedure first makes the app a well-defined automation of a working system instead of the system being an underspecified app. Corollary: the first playtest runs **Mode B** regardless of app progress — it exercises the prompt templates (needed anyway) and doubles as requirements-gathering for the app (whatever the GM finds tedious to log by hand is the app's feature list).

How active skills modify Force, add tactical bonuses, or create special Clash conditions; class Signature Skills and Principle Applications need the unified treatment (Stable Abilities were cut 2026-08-06). Prerequisite for the class framework's Signature Skill shape. **Design shape (2026-08-02, adopted from external review): a closed ability grammar.** Every generated or hand-built ability assembles from a fixed component list: trigger, target/range, governing Attribute, Beat cost, Aether cost + origin Grade, frequency gate, bonus size + stacking tag (priced off the Modifier Budget), duration, drawback/limitation, narrative envelope (Application Grade). The System AI names, flavors, and combines components; it never invents new ones. This becomes the spine of the class framework's Signature Skill spec and every generation prompt template.

## Pending Design Decisions (Not in Current Cycle)

- **Exceptional Success — RESOLVED 2026-08-01 ("the Merchant's Daughter problem").** Adopted as proposed: natural 96–100 on a skill check, no cascade, no math; success narrates a step beyond what was asked, failure is Soft regardless of margin. Both ends of the natural die now talk (01–05 Catastrophic, 96–100 Exceptional), 96 doubles as the F-Grade explosion threshold, and the fiction stays clean: Exceptional Success is mortal skill, explosions are the System. In `10` (Failure Consequences) and `75`.
- ~~**Volatility restructure**~~ **RESOLVED 2026-08-01 — see Recently Resolved.**
- **(superseded text follows, kept for context)** Gabriel's read-through: high-Grade explosion rates (46% B, 61% S) feel tedious (roll-again plus hard mental addition like 97+38) and cheapen crits; he wants explosions rare, epic, and the thresholds memorable without the book. His other itch — a surgery check exploding — is a real leak: "Resistance Rolls made during combat" currently explode, so mid-combat skill checks crit. Claude's proposal on the table: (a) restrict Volatility to Clash rolls, combat Will Saves, and the Breakthrough Check (skill checks never explode, even mid-combat); (b) rethreshold to **96 minus 1 per Grade above F** (F 96+/5%, E 95+/6%, D 94+/7%, C 93+/8%, B 92+/9%, S 91+/10%), keeping crits d20-rare at every Grade while still doubling by S; memorable as "starts at 96, drops one per Grade." Knock-ons if adopted: Breakthrough Check explosion odds text in `30`, the 2+ cascade Battle Memory faucet becomes very rare (acceptable; other triggers exist), quick-ref table, design-invariants memory.
- **Application power vs. acquisition Grade — RESOLVED 2026-08-01, implemented as "Application Grade" in the Principles chapter (see Recently Resolved).** Original question, kept for context: a C-Grade-acquired Seed costs 10,000 Aether while the same-tier F-Seed costs 10 — is the C-Seed more powerful, or the same skill at a 1000× price? Current text: same table effect (+10 is Grade-agnostic), price differs as a pool-fraction balancer, which is genuinely weird on its face. Claude's proposed resolution: **Applications carry a Grade** (set at acquisition or Refinement) that fixes both their Aether cost and their potency envelope — the narrative scale of the manifestation (what Searing Strike can burn through, how much space an AoE covers), while the Clash bonus stays inside the Modifier Budget at every Grade. Old cheap skills are old small skills; Refinement during Compression can re-forge an Application at current Grade (new cost, new envelope), which is what "real power comes from new-tier skills" actually means. Also note: Gabriel's instinct to gate Principle tiers by Grade is already implemented (tier caps, Principles chapter) — he'll meet it when he reads chapter 4.
- **Alpha-strike Momentum lethality (playtest flag, 2026-08-01).** Winning initial Momentum lets a whole side act first with fat margins; at Levels 1–3 the losing side can be Downed before acting. Dampeners already in the rules: Downed-instead-of-dead, Turned Aside, defensive explosions. Candidate structural fix if playtests confirm the problem: an escalation round (round 1, every character has 1 Beat) — Gabriel likes it structurally but rejects it as a special case to memorize; revisit only with playtest evidence.

- **Playtest watch list (2026-08-02, consolidated from the external-review triage).** Measure, don't pre-fix: (1) alpha-strike Momentum (flag above); (2) does the HVE Coherence bonus induce monotone archetype-performance at the table; (3) does uncapped percent-title stacking strain the d100 by late F-Grade; (4) Beat-granting titles: hard cap rejected as special-casing (Gabriel, 2026-08-02) — the discipline is to hand out third-Beat effects rarely and never write two that stack; (5) HVE inter-rater check: give ~20 logged moments to two or three readers, compare axis and intensity assignments; (6) combat engine metrics: peer fights resolve in 3–5 rounds without GM thumb, Momentum helps but doesn't decide, defense/movement/setup/healing each earn their page count, boss durability comes from mechanics beyond HP; (7) pill economy without Toxin (cut 2026-08-04): the 1-Beat action, Pill Saturation diminishing returns, and pricing should hold; watch for out-of-combat chug patterns that need a price or rarity answer.
- **Armor design pass (deferred by choice, 2026-08-01).** Keep no-DR and armor-as-Clash-modifier; bonuses live in the +1 to +10 band for the same compression reasons as Proficiency. When armor gets designed (naturally, alongside more items), the design space is tradeoff axes: DEX penalties, posture restrictions (FOR-absorb only), noise/stealth, weight vs. Free Step. The existing Low-Grade Armor Scraps item (+5 FOR-defense, −5 DEX Clashes) is the pattern to generalize.

- **Energy Density as a full subsystem:** The Breakthrough doc defines energy density tiers sufficient for Breakthroughs and Cultivation defines passive absorption rates, but the broader subsystem (affecting Principle resonance, monster spawning, territorial control, hex-level mapping) needs its own section.
- **Higher-Grade Breakthrough themes (D→C and beyond):** The universal blueprint and formula extend, but thematic trial content, failure severity, and Transcendent reward scales need development when the campaign reaches that point. Pocketed idea (Gabriel, 2026-08-03): a D→C or C-Grade Breakthrough might require extended meditation, the thousand-year-contemplation trope compressed into playable form.
- **Bloodline interaction with Breakthroughs:** How does an active Bloodline modify the trial? Additional internal challenge, or a shortcut?
- **Large-negative-margin outcomes and defensive explosion payoffs.** Volatility applies symmetrically — a winning defender's exploded roll can drive the attacker's Margin sharply negative. Currently: a winning defender deals no damage. Open question is whether large-negative-margin outcomes should produce mechanical consequences beyond "no damage" — counter-damage, a forced Momentum shift, weapon-breakage, a free Beat, or pure narrative spectacle. Needs playtesting before deciding.

- **Downed and Death rules locked (2026-07-18).** New "Downed and Death" subsection in `10-core-mechanics.md` (echoed in `05`, `60`, `20`, `25`).
