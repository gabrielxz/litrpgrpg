## Pending Design Decisions (Not in Current Cycle)

- **Energy Density as a full subsystem:** The Breakthrough doc defines energy density tiers sufficient for Breakthroughs and Cultivation defines passive absorption rates, but the broader subsystem (affecting Principle resonance, monster spawning, territorial control, hex-level mapping) needs its own section.
- **Higher-Grade Breakthrough themes (D→C and beyond):** The universal blueprint and formula extend, but thematic trial content, failure severity, and Transcendent reward scales need development when the campaign reaches that point.
- **Bloodline interaction with Breakthroughs:** How does an active Bloodline modify the trial? Additional internal challenge, or a shortcut?
- **Ability/Spell interaction with the Clash system:** How do active skills modify Force, add Tactical bonuses, or create special Clash conditions? Stable Abilities are a tutorial-tier example; class-tier and Principle-tier interactions need a unified treatment. (Touches Blocks A and B; pick up formally after both.)
- **Large-negative-margin outcomes and defensive explosion payoffs.** Volatility applies symmetrically — a winning defender's exploded roll can drive the attacker's Margin sharply negative. Currently: a winning defender deals no damage. Open question is whether large-negative-margin outcomes should produce mechanical consequences beyond "no damage" — counter-damage, a forced Momentum shift, weapon-breakage, a free Beat, or pure narrative spectacle. Needs playtesting before deciding.

## Recently Resolved

- **Aether rename + em-dash/prose sweep; revision cycle complete (2026-07-08).** The character resource "Energy" is now **Aether** across all chapters (Max Aether = Raw POW, Aether Pills, Aether costs, bestiary stat lines); "Volatile Energy"/VE and ambient "Energy Density" deliberately kept their names. All ~420 em-dashes removed from the 14 published chapters via sentence restructuring (periods, commas, colons, semicolons, parentheses; table "n/a" placeholder dashes became words); en-dashes, minus signs, and arrows untouched; verified zero remaining. Concurrent prose-anti-pattern cleanup per CLAUDE.md: cut "This is intentional"/"a feature, not a bug" (30), "Numbers go up. Players feel it." tagline (70), "pair beautifully"/"These are good." (55), "Slogs are the enemy of the design." (60), "deliberate randomizers" (65), "That is intentional" (15), "time-to-kill drops sharply" and a Momentum tagline (10); "System Volatility replaces traditional critical hits" reworded to answer the crit question directly. Borderline passages (in-world lore closers, Design Notes framing, GM calibration lines) deliberately kept. Also fixed a Defense Force redundancy in `10` (physical defense was described twice after the Batch 1 posture fix).

- **Block C — stat economy & Volatility spice; Block C complete (2026-07-08).** (1) **Breakthrough Check reformulated:** now `d100 + HRT Force + Force of your highest Attribute` (was POW + HRT). HRT is the universal willpower anchor; the second slot expresses the build — brawlers Break Through as body-cultivators on STR, mages on POW. Fixes POW being mandatory for every archetype at the game's most important roll; no DC recalibration needed (still two Forces, max natural 298). POW's other roles (Aether pool, spell Force, symmetric VE stats) deliberately unchanged. Updated in `30` (formula, DC analysis, summary card) and `25`. (2) **The Breakthrough Check is Volatility-live** — the one non-combat roll that explodes, at the character's current Grade threshold (5% at F→E rising to ~21% at D→C; tribulations grow wilder with Grade). Exception noted in `10`'s Volatility section. (3) **Cascades of 2+ extra dice on a PC's roll auto-grant a Battle Memory Card** — resolves the old "sustained cascades" backlog item by feeding the IP economy rather than gating progression; in `10`, `20`, and `05`. (4) **Aura Pressure intervention:** stays roll-free but the intervener must not themselves be Suppressed (kills the suppressed-chain reroll exploit); in `10`.

- **Block B — Principles chapter rebuilt; Block B complete (2026-07-02).** `20-principles.md` rewritten end-to-end on the agreed skeleton. One ladder (Initial Insight → Seed → Early/Mid/Peak Fragment) with a real IP economy: thresholds 3/10/25/50/100 cumulative; awards Battle Memory 1–3 / affinity treasure 1–2 / vision 1 / life-or-death 2; IP accrues only from Concept-aligned experiences; **IP totals are player-visible** (systemvoice notification), the forged Concept is not. **Compression** is the tier-up ritual (articulate the pattern; GM/System refines); **Refinement** steers a Concept at same tier/slot/IP. **Concept slots:** 1 at F +1 per Breakthrough (= the Breakthrough doc's "new Principle slots"). **Tier caps:** F→Seed, E→Mid Fragment, D→Peak Fragment; IP banks past a cap, Compression waits for Breakthrough. **New Concepts always enter at 0 IP** regardless of Grade, priced at origin Grade (explains the cost chart's C-Grade Seed row); mastery never transfers. **Fusion mechanized:** during Compression, d100 + PER Force vs. Severe (140); success = fused Concept at lower parent's tier in one slot (frees a slot); failure = higher parent −10 IP, locked until next Breakthrough; fused Applications price at peak Modifier Budget but natural 01–05 = Catastrophic Failure. Ontology's parallel ladder and Domains-taxonomy cut; its System-ontology material condensed to a closing section. Worked progression example added (Kara → Weight). **Stable Abilities kept** — with zero-Principle creation, the Stable Ability is the only active hook before ~Level 5 and the HVE's first visible output.

- **Block A part 3 — quick hits; Block A complete (2026-07-02).** (1) **Proficiency package:** flat +10 kept; added **Routine Mastery** (with a relevant Proficiency, auto-succeed Trivial/Easy tasks of your own Grade — subsumes and replaces the old Passive Awareness paragraph) and **Specialist Gating** (some tasks cannot be attempted unskilled; GM decides). Skilled vs. unskilled is now access + reliability, not modifier size. (2) **Anti-Repetition rule cut** — it taxed the Force archetype's honest baseline and polluted HVE signal; variety incentives now come from Momentum/Advantaged/Turned Aside. (3) **Beat counts canonicalized:** default 2 for everyone; stat blocks may override; third-Beat grants are the rarest form of power and always explicit. (4) **Modifier Budget table added** to the Clash's Tactical Modifiers: +5 minor / +10 standard / +15–20 peak-and-rare; environment −10 hindering / −20 crippling; new content prices bonuses against it. All in `10-core-mechanics.md`, with the environment line echoed in `05-quick-reference.md`.

- **Block A part 2: Turned Aside — defense pays in tempo (2026-07-02).** Deliberately no damage reduction (subtraction step would break one-roll Clash resolution and cross-Grade scaling). Instead: a defender who wins the Clash by Margin 20+ leaves the attacker **Exposed** until the end of their next turn; a defensive win containing a Volatility explosion also counts as a Decisive Tactical Reversal (Momentum shift). Applies to every defensive posture, works cross-Grade (margins are margins). Counter-damage remains deferred to playtesting per the large-negative-margin backlog item — Turned Aside is the minimal committed version. Also fixed a real inconsistency found during implementation: the core-mechanics Grade Gap worked example gave the F-Peak 99 HP ("instantly dead") while the quick reference's identical example had FOR 99 → 198 HP surviving at 68; core mechanics now matches the quick reference and states explicitly that FOR/HP is the survive-the-gap stat.

- **Block A part 1: movement, position, and tempo (2026-07-02).** Four interlocking rules landed in `10-core-mechanics.md` and `05-quick-reference.md`. (1) **Free Step:** DEX Force 50+ grants one free Zone move or in-Zone reposition per turn (does not bypass Engagement). Single tier by design — faster-movement powers beyond it belong to Principles (e.g., free Disengage). (2) **Cross-Grade movement is absolute:** movement contests vs. lower Grades auto-succeed unless the fiction intervenes; in combat, +1 free Zone move per turn per Grade above the highest-Grade hostile. Resolves the former front-of-backlog DEX deal-breaker. (3) **Advantaged replaces Elevated:** the +10 positional state is now any fiction-derived edge, assigned per-combatant (high ground for an archer, stone underfoot for an Earth cultivator). Exposed unchanged (ability/item hooks preserved). (4) **Momentum Rolls are d100 + DEX or PER Force, whichever is higher** — applies to Initial Momentum, Multi-Faction order, and Seize Momentum; each side's roller is its highest DEX-or-PER combatant.
