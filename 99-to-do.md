File a US trademark application for GRADEBREAKER (intent-to-use is fine before release) in the classes covering printed games and books, downloadable game materials, and entertainment services, after the clearance search. Register the text of each released version. Two wrinkles to raise with the attorney: (1) the AI-generated art is likely not copyrightable under current US Copyright Office guidance, and registrations must disclaim AI-generated material; (2) the co-designed text needs human authorship on the record, which the git history and the backlog already document in detail. No license means all rights reserved by default and no permission for the community content Stage 2 wants; pick one and print it in the front matter.

    - **Genre limits.** The System, Grades, Breakthroughs, and the LitRPG conventions belong to the genre and cannot be owned by anyone; the defensible expression is the specific text, the art direction, the name, and the trade dress.

## Naming (decided 2026-09-10)

**The game is Gradebreaker.** One word, capital G; "Grade Breaker" is tolerated informally and never appears in our own materials. Cover block: **GRADEBREAKER / The LitRPG RPG / Power is not granted.** The subtitle and the tagline are locked (the "A LitRPG: RPG" variant and "Break through. Become more." are dead). The tagline was cut from the Principles chapter on 2026-09-20 and is parked. **No engine name.** "Magnitude, the system Gradebreaker runs on" was proposed and dropped the same day: "magnitude" is already a plain noun in the rules text and the name of the Magnitude Gap Rule, so an engine called Magnitude would make one word carry three meanings. Rejected titles: Overgrade, Magnitude, Tenfold, Ungranted, Integrant, and the whole Sovereign / Threshold / Index / Ascendant / Vector family; Threshold is doubly dead, since a funded Gamefound board game of that name sits on the same shelf and Volatility Threshold is a rules term.

**Clearance status.** Registry check (itch, DriveThru, BGG) clean per Gabriel, 2026-09-10; a web search the same day found no game, novel, or board game named Gradebreaker. **USPTO clearance still pending before the Kickstarter.** The LITRPG word mark (Reg. 5377086, Aleron Kong, registered 2018-01-09) shows as cancelled under Section 8 on 2024-07-22, which retires most of the 2026-08-08 concern about the subtitle; confirm on TSDR during the Gradebreaker search, since a cancelled mark can be refiled. **Domains:** gradebreaker.com is registered to Gabriel (2026-09-17). gradebreakerrpg.com and gradebreaker.games were unregistered on 2026-09-10 and are deliberately not held; `.rpg` is not a delegated top-level domain.

How active skills modify Force, add tactical bonuses, or create special Clash conditions; class Signature Skills and Principle Applications need the unified treatment (Stable Abilities were cut 2026-08-06). Prerequisite for the class framework's Signature Skill shape. **Design shape (2026-08-02, adopted from external review): a closed ability grammar.** Every generated or hand-built ability assembles from a fixed component list: trigger, target/range, governing Attribute, Beat cost, Aether cost + origin Grade, frequency gate, bonus size + stacking tag (priced off the Modifier Budget), duration, drawback/limitation, narrative envelope (Application Grade). The System AI names, flavors, and combines components; it never invents new ones. This becomes the spine of the class framework's Signature Skill spec and every generation prompt template.
11. **Glossary + internal-link pass + link CSS — one feature, promoted from Tier 3 (2026-07-31).** Promoted because Gabriel's read-through of the current build keeps hitting forward references (the example of play uses "soft failure," "Turned Aside," and defense postures pages before their definitions), and because the delivery format already supports the fix: the EPUB is HTML underneath, internal links survive Send-to-Kindle conversion, and the build feeds all chapters through Pandoc as one document so `[Turned Aside](#turned-aside)`-style anchors resolve across chapters. One tap for a reminder, two for the full rule; degrades perfectly to print with no links at all.
    - **Link on first use per chapter,** never every occurrence.
    - **Whisper styling:** EPUB CSS renders links as plain text with a faint dotted underline, no color; one line in the Introduction tells readers rule terms link to the glossary.
    - Glossary doubles as a terminology consistency check during writing.

## Worldbuilding (gating; started 2026-09-12)

**Started 2026-09-12.** Gabriel's tutorial re-read (first 20 pages: happy overall, tweaks to format, details, and story coming) surfaced that the lore pass has to happen before the tutorial is finalized, because the tutorial is where players first meet the world and its hooks should point at real facts (his example: the alien Initiates should be a race the galaxy knows). The worksheet is `lore/questions.md`: Parts A and B (the tutorial's needs, the System) first, C through E after, F is Claude's delivery. Gabriel answers; Claude writes from the answers.

The setting is currently generic-LitRPG on purpose, but it needs enough flesh that a GM has material to reach for while players stay free to tell any story.

**Ordered in three stages, reordered 2026-08-15 by Gabriel.** The voice used to be one item among several. The Initiate's Manual is written entirely in it, so a whole book now depends on it being settled, and it goes first.

**Stage 1: the System's voice.** A worked-out personality: menacing but leaning inscrutable, carrying a little character without becoming GLaDOS. Deliverables are a written characterization, a set of rules for what the System does and does not say, and enough sample messages across registers (notification, quest, warning, recognition, refusal) that someone else could write in it. The Introduction's opening System message gets rewritten here ("Tutorial mercy: limited" is the wrong kind of fake-menace; "Mortality: active" implies an off switch), and it can grow a sentence or two if there is something good to say. The "What the System Wants" section in The System AI is the seed to deepen. Also settle here: whether the voice shifts by Grade, since the Manual is an F-Grade document and would inherit whatever that decision is.

**Blocks:** the Initiate's Manual, every `systemvoice` box in the book, and the System personality question in the run modes.

**Stage 2: what the voice speaks about.** A distinct sensory identity for Aether (feel, smell, sound), System history, legends, and notable leaders, wars, and locations. The VE death-motes line in Cultivation is Aether sensory anchor #1 and the intoxication-of-first-contact note (R22) is #2. The cosmology poster draws from this stage, so it is gated here rather than on Stage 1.

**Stage 2 carries a standing requirement, set by Gabriel 2026-08-15: the System and the multiverse's systems must be weird in specific, grounded ways.**

His model is what actually happened with LLMs. They took over the modern world on the strength of genuinely incredible abilities and are, at the same time, deeply strange in ways almost nobody predicted: sycophantic, shaky on truth against fiction, bad at arithmetic, unable to count the letters in a word, and steerable past a refusal by asking for a Python script that prints the thing instead. **He does not want those specific quirks ported over.** He wants that texture: superhuman capability sitting beside failures that are surprising, specific, and consequential.

**The test, and the reason his examples work.** Each LLM quirk follows from the architecture. Tokenization explains the letter counting. Training toward approval explains the flattery. Next-token prediction explains the confident falsehood. A thin persona over a general simulator explains the jailbreak. Every one is obvious in retrospect once the mechanism is known. So: **no quirk goes in unless it follows from something true about what the System is in this universe, and that underlying fact should be discoverable in play.** Weirdness for its own sake fails the test. Weirdness a player can eventually explain is the target.

Scope is the world, the System itself, and the systems of the multiverse generally, so this is not only a voice note. It should also produce quirks with mechanical hooks rather than pure flavor. The book already has one of exactly this kind, which is the precedent: the System's difficulty ratings are its best guess and are sometimes miscalibrated.

**The governing fact, ruled by Gabriel 2026-08-15: the System is not omniscient. It is fallible and it makes mistakes.** Every quirk below is a way that shows.

**Adopted (Gabriel, 2026-08-15).** Strong concepts, hard to implement well, and he is going in on all four.

- **It measures energy and infers everything else.** A sleeping man and a corpse read alike at low resolution. Exact about anything it priced, approximate about everything else. His own worked delivery, and the register he wants for this material: a found log reading *"the system gave me a mission to retrieve an artifact from a lifeless cave, but there was a sleeping monster in it. I guess it thought sleeping was the same thing as dead."*
- **It can be addressed, and it wants things.** The source-fiction anchor is *Defiance of the Fall*, where the System has wants and Zack negotiates with it. **Use sparingly.** Direct demands fail; a proposal in a format it recognizes sometimes lands.
- **Its history has holes it does not notice**, and it answers confidently across them. Mechanical hook Gabriel wants: **quests that recruit players to reconstruct lost knowledge.** The care he flagged: the reason the System cannot recover it alone has to hold up. His own opening answer is that observation is its main thing but it can carry secondary goals.
- **Old sectors run old behavior.** Version drift across the multiverse, with no update having reached some places.

**Rejected (Gabriel, 2026-08-15).** **Over-affirmation.** It fits the System's mission and it lands too close to the LLM it was drawn from. Recorded so it does not return by another road: the family of quirks to avoid is the one where a reader recognizes the specific chatbot behavior rather than the world.

**Still open, neither adopted nor cut.** Its Title vocabulary being a closed set, so a novel act files under the nearest existing label and the label then shapes what the character becomes. Being precise about magnitude and vague about kind. Being unable to say why, since it reports rather than reasons.

**Delivery, ruled by Gabriel 2026-08-15: spread across several vectors, and the players learn indirectly.** The GM is told plainly somewhere; a player should arrive at it by noticing. Vectors available, several of which already exist as separate plans:

- **Found logs and the dead cultivator's journal**, which is his own example and the strongest register for this.
- **The Initiate's Manual is already specified as subtly wrong in two or three places.** Those errors now have a cause rather than being a conceit: the Manual is wrong because the System is wrong in these specific ways, so a table that catches an error in the Manual has caught a quirk. Whichever quirks land should be the Manual's planted errors.
- **The survivors' forum handout** (brainstorm 2026-08-15), where people compare notes on strange System behavior, which is how a real community would find these.
- **Quest and Mandate text that misdescribes its own objective**, the sleeping-cave shape.
- **Bestiary entries whose System classification does not match the creature.**
- **A `lore` vignette**, and NPC dialogue.

**Ruled 2026-08-26: lost knowledge is adopted as the System's angle.** The embodiment answer stands: **VE is Aether still wearing the shape of whatever held it last** (2026-08-12), so information rides on energy that has been through bodies, and the System reads residue but cannot travel to it. Its limit is embodiment rather than intelligence. It has no hands, which is why it issues quests instead of acting, and players are the hands. It also retroactively explains the genre convention of Systems that ask.

**Overturned 2026-09-19 (Gabriel, via the lore doc):** the no-hands limit is retired. The System could execute most of what it asks for and asks anyway, because watching independent cultivators attempt, choose, and invent teaches it more than running a known procedure. Lost-knowledge quests survive under the new frame. The book carried no no-hands prose, so nothing was edited. The same session ruled the three open quirks: precise-about-magnitude is adopted as a restatement of measures-energy; the closed Title vocabulary and unable-to-say-why are cut. Stage 1 (the voice) is largely settled in `lore/setting.md`: one register across Grades, the attention shift at a death, the humor rules; the voice section in The System AI is the remaining deliverable. Stage 2 (what it speaks about) has its facts; Stage 3 (delivery) is the worksheet's list.

**Watch: wanting versus judging.** R2 moved the Introduction from a System that *decides* what you are becoming to one that *observes*, and the HVE chapter's stance is that it records conduct without judging it. A System with wants is compatible with that, since wanting an artifact recovered is not an opinion about who you are, but the line should be drawn deliberately rather than discovered later.

**Available, if version drift is adopted properly:** it composes with diegetic errata (brainstorm 2026-08-15), where balance changes ship as in-fiction System patches. An old sector that never received the patch is a sector running the previous edition's rules, which a GM could run as written.

**Stage 3: delivery into the book.** The color vignettes (Tier 3 item 14) carry most of it. Gabriel wants the Quests chapter and the Volatile Artifacts section each opened with a story, ideally related ones; the strong version is one recurring survivor whose story threads every chapter opener. The Breakthrough gift box and the marked chapter story slots land here.

## The Extra Mile: Companion Artifacts and Reach Goals

*From a brainstorm on 2026-08-15. The question was what a solo designer working with AI can add that telegraphs love for the genre and gives players something they did not know they wanted. Gabriel's picks are below with his framing; the ideas he passed over are not recorded, so a re-brainstorm would need to start fresh.*

**A Kickstarter is now in the picture** as a release vehicle, which it was not before. Several items below are explicitly stretch goals or high-tier backer rewards rather than book content, and that is a new category for this backlog.

### Committed: The Initiate's Manual (high priority)

A second, thinner, player-facing book written in the System's voice and issued in-fiction to newly Integrated humans. It teaches the Clash, Beats, Grades, Consolidation, and the resources. **It does not mention the Hidden Vector Engine at all**, because the System does not show a player that. It is subtly wrong in two or three places, in ways a table discovers in play. The GM book stays the truth.

Why it fits: the "What Can Be Seen" chapter already defines what the System reveals and withholds, and the character sheet is already the diegetic System Interface. The Manual is those two decisions taken to their conclusion as a physical object.

**Sequencing, per Gabriel 2026-08-15:** it goes **after the worldbuilding pass**, because the System's voice and personality are settled there and the Manual is written entirely in that voice. It **ships with the playtest kit (Milestone 2)**, which is a scope addition to that milestone. The dependency is on **Stage 1 of the worldbuilding pass** (the voice) rather than on the whole pass, and the pass was reordered the same day to put the voice first for exactly this reason.

Open when it starts: how long (a pamphlet or a real book), whether the deliberate errors are listed for the GM in an appendix or left for the GM to find, whether it carries the Table Kit sheets or the main book keeps them, and whether it is a separate PDF or a section of the same file with its own build target.

**Updated 2026-08-15:** the deliberate errors are no longer free choices. The System's adopted quirks (worldbuilding Stage 2) are what the Manual should be wrong about, so catching an error in the Manual is how a table discovers a quirk. This also settles the appendix question in favor of listing them for the GM, since the GM has to be told plainly what the players are meant to find indirectly.

### Kickstarter stretch goals and high-tier rewards

- **The marginalia edition.** The rulebook as a used copy annotated by a previous owner, an Integrated human getting stronger as the pages go: corrections, a crossed-out passage with "this is wrong, I've seen it happen," a dead friend's name. The handwriting changes around the Breakthrough chapter, and somewhere in the back third the notes stop. Gabriel: a stretch goal or high-tier reward. Grayscale-safe by construction, so it survives the Scribe. AI is what makes a consistent 233-page character voice achievable for one person.
- **Volatility dice.** d100 sets with the threshold band marked, one die per Grade, so the number that means the die betrayed you is physically visible. Gabriel: "100% Kickstarter stretch or high tier goal."
- **Achievement stickers.** A peel-and-stick sheet for titles. Matches the achievement-layer tone Gabriel set for titles, and it sidesteps the title-card adhesive problem by going on a sleeve rather than the sheet. Same tier as the dice.
- **The fold-out cosmology poster.** The Grade ladder, the tiers above Domain, the Axioms, as one map that mostly says "you do not know yet." **Gated on the worldbuilding pass**, since Gabriel wants it grounded in the real setting rather than invented for the poster.
- **Personalized pregens.** A backer answers a handful of questions and receives a character sheet built for them, with a title the System "already" assigned. Gabriel's angle, and the reason he likes it: **you play yourself**. Worth noting that this is the one reward that is also a design probe, since it tests whether Behavioral Stat Mapping can read a real person from answers rather than from play.

### Maybe

- **Solo mode.** An AI-run GM for one player, shipped as procedures plus a prompt pack. Gabriel: "really ambitious," logged as a maybe. The argument for it is that the genre's audience are solitary readers, and this is the bridge from reading the fiction to playing it. The argument against is that it is a second product wearing a chapter's clothing.

### New design thread: hidden and asymmetric quests

Gabriel's response to a proposal that the System could test one player using another player's logged behavior. His reference is **Nemesis**, where a player can hold a hidden goal aimed at another player.

**The constraint he set: do not incentivize PVP much.** The space he wants explored is ultra-personalized quests, board-game-shaped, where the System hands one player an objective the rest of the table cannot see.

Raw material this already has: Personal Opportunities are private by construction and already run one player at a time with no group consultation; the HVE gives the System a private read on every player, so it can aim a quest at exactly the axis a character is weakest on; and quest sharing (2026-08-14) established that the System re-prices objectives at the moment of sharing, which implies it knows who holds what.

Questions before designing: what a hidden objective can ask for short of harming another PC (betray a confidence, withhold aid for one round, take the thing they wanted, answer honestly when asked); whether the target ever learns; whether a hidden objective can be declined the way a Personal Opportunity can; and what stops the table from simply asking each other. Watch the HVE signal quality, which is the reason to want this at all: a private objective produces the cleanest behavioral data the game can generate.

### Wanted: a tropes chapter

Name the moves the genre runs on and give each a mechanical hook: the tutorial that kills most of the class, weakest-to-strongest, the mentor who is a fragment, the sect that is a scam, the System message that arrives at the worst moment. Gabriel saw a Reddit post cataloguing these and loved it. Sits naturally beside the bibliography and the recursion essay in the front or back matter.

## Pending Design Decisions (Not in Current Cycle)

- **The Gradebreaker title (stub, 2026-09-10; do not design yet).** A System-granted title named for the game, awarded for defeating an opponent a full Grade above the character, under conditions to be defined; it should carry real mechanical weight. Two facts for whoever designs it: (1) the trigger coincides with a guaranteed collapse (an E-Grade kill pays an F-Grade character 100 to 500 VE against a Critical line of 240), so the title lands on someone waking from an involuntary Consolidation, which is the right scene; (2) "mechanical weight" means Hidden Achievement class, since Achievement titles are flat +1 to +5 only under the 2026-08-13 retune, and Giant-Feller (Severe or Peak kill, +2 FOR) is the rung below it in the catalog. The "specific build path" principle cited in the naming memo is not in the book; the book's actual texture is that a full-Grade-up kill is a party-wide gamble the +100 makes near-impossible by default. Sits beside the Grade-differentiation question (CLUSTER-D in `archive/backlog-resolved.md`).
- **Heart needs a job (Gabriel, 2026-09-10).** Tolerance went flat, so HRT no longer decides anything every session; what remains is mental and spiritual defense, Aura Pressure saves, the Fusion roll, and the Breakthrough Check. Gabriel's position: a stat that only saves you from bad things is not fun, and the 08-12 ruling still stands in spirit (a Breakthrough-mandated stat has to pay off along the way). Brainstorm later; constraint from the same day's rulings is that the job must not touch the VE tank.
- **Run-mode names, no letters (2026-09-10).** The book names the three run modes Unplugged, AI-Assisted, and the Companion App, in that order; it never assigns letters. One stray "Mode A / Mode B" in the HVE chapter was removed. The backlog's older Mode A/B/C shorthand (A = app, B = AI-Assisted, C = Unplugged) is internal history only and must not leak into the book.
- **Mini-map / System map UI — declined for now (2026-08-07).** Map-reveal quest rewards stay narrative; revisit if the table wants cartography.

- **Alpha-strike Momentum lethality (playtest flag, 2026-08-01).** Winning initial Momentum lets a whole side act first with fat margins; at Levels 1–3 the losing side can be Downed before acting. Dampeners already in the rules: Downed-instead-of-dead, Turned Aside, defensive explosions. Candidate structural fix if playtests confirm the problem: an escalation round (round 1, every character has 1 Beat) — Gabriel likes it structurally but rejects it as a special case to memorize; revisit only with playtest evidence.

- **Playtest watch list (2026-08-02, consolidated from the external-review triage).** Measure, don't pre-fix: (1) alpha-strike Momentum (flag above); (2) does the HVE Coherence bonus induce monotone archetype-performance at the table; (3) does uncapped percent-title stacking strain the d100 by late F-Grade; (4) Beat-granting titles: hard cap rejected as special-casing (Gabriel, 2026-08-02) — the discipline is to hand out third-Beat effects rarely and never write two that stack; (5) HVE inter-rater check: give ~20 logged moments to two or three readers, compare axis and intensity assignments; (6) combat engine metrics: peer fights resolve in 3–5 rounds without GM thumb, Momentum helps but doesn't decide, defense/movement/setup/healing each earn their page count, boss durability comes from mechanics beyond HP; (7) pill economy without Toxin (cut 2026-08-04): the 1-Beat action, Pill Saturation diminishing returns, and pricing should hold; watch for out-of-combat chug patterns that need a price or rarity answer.
- **Firearms have a Proficiency and no weapon (parked by Gabriel, 2026-08-14).** The Fighting domain split gave firearms its own slot, deliberately, because Gabriel expects to introduce System-forged or otherwise strange guns later and wants somewhere for them to land. Nothing points at it yet: the F-Grade weapon table has no pistol, rifle, or shotgun row, and the book has no ammunition rule. A player may take the Proficiency today and own nothing it applies to, which is acceptable for now. What a decision needs: whether mundane Earth guns work at all after Integration and for how long (the genre answer is that they decay into irrelevance as bodies outscale steel), whether ammunition is tracked or abstracted into scarcity, and what the System-forged version does that a bow does not. Touches the Items weapon table, Character Creation's sample list, and whatever the worldbuilding pass decides about the state of Earth's arsenals. **Resolved 2026-09-19 (rules 0.1.4):** guns work and are firearms-domain weapons like any other shape (DEX, Trained +5, adjacent Zones, damage as Margin); a found firearm holds 1d10 rounds, ammunition exists wherever Earth left it and nothing in the book makes more, and an empty gun is an improvised object; artillery, vehicle weapons, and explosives have no weapon shape and are improvised objects. Temper, the lore name for the Grade multiplier as ordinary force feels it, is one paragraph in the Weapons section. Still open: whether Earth guns decay in relevance faster than Temper alone implies, and System-forged guns as items.
- **Armor design pass (deferred by choice, 2026-08-01).** Keep no-DR and armor-as-Clash-modifier; bonuses live in the +1 to +10 band for the same compression reasons as Proficiency. When armor gets designed (naturally, alongside more items), the design space is tradeoff axes: DEX penalties, posture restrictions (FOR-absorb only), noise/stealth, weight vs. Free Step. The existing Low-Grade Armor Scraps item (+5 FOR-defense, −5 DEX Clashes) is the pattern to generalize.

- **Energy Density as a full subsystem:** The Breakthrough doc defines energy density tiers sufficient for Breakthroughs and Cultivation defines passive absorption rates, but the broader subsystem (affecting Principle resonance, monster spawning, territorial control, hex-level mapping) needs its own section.
- **Higher-Grade Breakthrough themes (D→C and beyond):** The universal blueprint and formula extend, but thematic trial content, failure severity, and Transcendent reward scales need development when the campaign reaches that point. Pocketed idea (Gabriel, 2026-08-03): a D→C or C-Grade Breakthrough might require extended meditation, the thousand-year-contemplation trope compressed into playable form.
- **Bloodline interaction with Breakthroughs:** How does an active Bloodline modify the trial? Additional internal challenge, or a shortcut?
- **Large-negative-margin outcomes and defensive explosion payoffs.** Volatility applies symmetrically — a winning defender's exploded roll can drive the attacker's Margin sharply negative. Currently: a winning defender deals no damage. Open question is whether large-negative-margin outcomes should produce mechanical consequences beyond "no damage" — counter-damage, a forced Momentum shift, weapon-breakage, a free Beat, or pure narrative spectacle. Needs playtesting before deciding.
