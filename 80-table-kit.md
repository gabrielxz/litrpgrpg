# Table Kit

The pages that follow are the print kit: the F-Grade character sheet, front and back, the GM's HVE sweep sheet, the optional bookkeeper's ledger, the three ready-made characters as chooser cards and as filled sheets ready to play, Battle Memory Cards, Title cards, and the tutorial's probe and Personal Opportunity cards. Photocopy them from here, or print the standalone kit PDF distributed alongside this book. The character sheet is one page per player, printed front and back; the card pages print on card stock and cut apart.

Title cards attach to nothing. A title arrives by being handed over and releases by being taken back, so the cards tuck between the sheet and a clear sleeve, or hang from a binder ring through the punched corner.
