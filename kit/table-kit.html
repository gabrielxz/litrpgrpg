LITRPG:RPG Actions & Combat PLAYER HANDOUT
[ YOUR TURN · TWO BEATS ]

One Beat buys: an attack · a spell · a move to an adjacent Zone · an item · a Principle Application · a skill check · claiming or stripping a Positional State · a Disengage · a Seize Momentum attempt.

Free: speaking, drawing a weapon, dropping something, drifting inside your Zone.

[ THE CLASH ]

Roll d100 + Force (+5 Trained, +10 Seasoned or Master). Both sides roll; high wins. The difference is the Margin, and at Grade F the Margin is the damage.

Defend with: DEX to dodge · FOR to absorb · HRT against mental · PER against illusion.

[ MODIFIERS ]
ADVANTAGED+10 to your rolls
EXPOSED−10 to your rolls
FLANKING+10 for every hostile on a shared target
HINDERING GROUND−10
SHIELD, DEFENDING+5, occupies a hand
[ ABOUT TO TAKE A HIT · YIELD ]

After the Margin is known, before damage lands: give up Beats from your next turn.

EACH BEAT−20 from the incoming Margin
TWO BEATSthrown one Zone, attacker’s choice, no free strike
CORNEREDnowhere to be driven: one Beat only
[ NEED IT TO LAND · SURGE ]

Spend half your Maximum Aether (round up) for +5 on one Clash roll you make. Declare it before rolling. No Beat. Stacks with everything.

Aether refills only at Consolidation, so this is one or two uses between rests.

[ THE DIE TALKS ]
NATURAL 96+, CLASHexplodes: roll again and add
NATURAL 96+, SKILL CHECKa step beyond what you asked for
EITHER WAYtick a Mark in the Proficiency you used
NATURAL 01–05catastrophic
[ ITEMS IN A FIGHT ]

1 Beat to use. Pills: the first two of each kind per fight work in full; more do nothing until ten quiet minutes out of combat. Shards: backfire on a natural 01–05.

[ AT 0 HP · DOWNED ]

No Beats, no defense. Dead at the end of your third round unless stabilized: any healing, or an ally’s 1 Beat and a Moderate (90) check. Survive it and the System hands you a Battle Memory.

[ AFTER THE FIGHT ]

Kills pay Volatile Energy into your tank; it becomes levels at Consolidation. Consolidation: each full hour clears a fifth of your Tolerance and restores a fifth of your Max HP; Aether refills at the first full hour. You are defenseless for the duration.
