# Gradebreaker — voice standard

Based on the accessible examples in “AI Voice Change Log,” not a reconstruction of its unavailable 16-item list.

Scope selected by the author: flag unclear prose and conspicuous dramatic padding; include optional edits for borderline cases. These are passage-level editorial tests, not claims about whether a person or AI wrote a sentence.

The main test: can the intended reader tell what happens, what to do, and why it matters without translating a dramatic phrase back into a procedure?

1. **Performed gravitas:** ominous pronouncements in explanatory prose. State the event, rule, or decision.
2. **Abstraction replacing an action:** name the actor, resource, event, or result. Refine VE, not 'the day.'
3. **Ornamental punchlines:** cut endings that add emphasis without meaning.
4. **False contrasts and non-answers:** replace 'nothing' followed by an objective, or 'not X but Y' without a useful distinction, with the actual information.
5. **Metaphors concealing procedures:** explain movement, timing, triggers, and outcomes literally before considering imagery.
6. **Ambiguous actors and referents:** distinguish player, character, GM, and in-world System; replace vague 'it,' 'them,' and passive agents where consequential.
7. **Absolute claims contradicted by exceptions:** narrow 'every,' 'always,' 'only,' 'nothing,' and numerical promises to what the rules support.
8. **Presentation imposing nonexistent symmetry:** do not group unlike counters or advancement systems as though identical.
9. **Compressed slogans where a rule is missing:** request an author decision rather than invent the missing mechanic in a polished replacement.
10. **Repetition of the premise:** explain behavioral recognition fully once; repeat only when it helps a specific procedure.

Keep: concrete sensory detail; dialogue that sounds like table conversation; clear metaphors that explain the game ('a build is a biography'); economical instructions; System text whose tone is intentionally impersonal, ominous, or overconfident. Epigraphs and read-aloud passages have different jobs from rules and GM instructions.

Suggested replacement text must preserve mechanical meaning. If meaning is uncertain, flag the question and provide a conditional replacement only after stating the assumption.
