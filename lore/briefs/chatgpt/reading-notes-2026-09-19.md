1. **Source cap:** Thirty candidates cannot fit five sources each appearing in at most four chapters. Each complete fifteen-chapter set instead uses each source three times; a final mixed selection needs its own cap check.
2. **Numbers and fictional knowledge:** Numerical permission in the old brief does not make a quantity part of the world. Gabriel's correction takes precedence: the revised epigraphs use only in-world observations and quantities, with no dice or character-creation knowledge. The shared voice still supplies the Introduction's fact.
3. **Public knowledge:** Introduction, Titles, and Grade Breakthroughs are treated as public despite the brief leaving their readership classification implicit. The epigraphs do not depend on the new sensory write-up or the proposed Kith explanation. The approved green-sweetness direction is ready to incorporate into the setting separately.
4. **Pending sources:** The document device is approved, but the five-source set is still `[proposal]`. These are reviewable candidates, not a claim that the source set has been approved.
5. **Legible errors:** A source's characteristic tone cannot establish that it is wrong. Proposed excerpts carry their own discrepancy or correction, with the GM text retaining the truth. The epigraph set contains no WRONG candidates, and Tutorial A is now separately marked PROPOSAL: the disputed count refers to returning people, and the absence of names is a suggested new historical detail.

## Relevant differences and tensions

- **Fragment Wraith:** The PDF calls it the dying coherence of a cultivator's mind (printed p. 152); the newer setting makes it a broken defensive effect. Hessar is the distinct conscious remnant. The drafts follow the setting and do not blur those categories.
- **Stonefold:** The PDF allows the unfinished stone to be taken for 20 VE (printed p. 188); the setting says interruption spoils it. The Bestiary candidate follows the setting and flags the encounter outcome for reconciliation.
- **Interpretation and return:** The older tutorial says translation will not be granted and leaves the far side of the gate open. The setting specifies Interpretation at registration, Earth late on Day 3, and the Kith beside the party.
- **Post-tutorial visitor:** The PDF offers an arriving higher-Grade entity as a Stinger option (printed p. 240). A physical visitor from an established world would now require the setting's bounded exception; the default Stinger should follow the paired gate and sky diagram.
- **System judgments:** Some old passages describe the System as judging or never explaining. The newer setting says it records tendencies without moral judgment and can sometimes explain. Refusal remains common; absolute inability to explain is not adopted.
- **VE and Aether:** Their identity as one substance in different states is explicit in the PDF's Aether section and in the focused brief, but is not a standalone fact in `setting.md`. Carry that premise into the setting when adopting the sensory facts.
- **Grade scaling:** "Everything scales ×10" must not become "Breakthrough multiplies every Attribute." The PDF expressly preserves Raw stats at ascension and changes caps, divisors, and Grade-dependent effects; the epigraphs avoid the blanket claim.
- **Name collision:** Tovan, the Kith fighter, and Toven, an illustrative Aru personal name, are hard to distinguish aloud. Consider dropping the illustrative name while retaining the established NPC.
