# Gradebreaker: chapter epigraph candidates

Handoff version incorporating Gabriel's feedback, 2026-09-19. These are two complete sets of candidates for editorial selection; the five-source set still awaits Gabriel's ruling. The quotations are newly written fictional excerpts, not quotations recovered from the supplied documents. Their factual content follows the supplied setting and rules, except for the explicitly proposed Ledger detail in Tutorial A.

**Source distribution:** The literal cap in the brief is impossible across all candidates: fifteen chapters times two candidates requires thirty source appearances, while five sources times four chapters allows twenty. Here A and B are independently balanced complete sets, each using every source exactly three times. A is the recommended starting set. Mixing candidates may require checking the four-chapter cap again.

**Scope:** The quotations concern things inhabitants can observe, experience, or read in their interfaces. Dice, roll modifiers, Margin, Resistance targets, point buy, and player character creation have no existence in the fiction. Visible Attributes, levels, Aether, and energy quantities can appear. No player-facing candidate exposes a `[GM]` or `[nobody]` fact; Introduction, Grade Breakthroughs, and Titles are also treated as public. None relies on the proposed sensory identity, Kith exception, return authorization, local System names, or paired Opportunities. TRUE describes the underlying fact, with anonymous anecdotes used to dramatize it; Tutorial A separately identifies a proposed historical detail.

## Editorial application

Anonymous incidents supply fictional presentation of the stated facts. They introduce no named contributor, new rule, or major historical event. Tutorial A specifies a disputed archival count of people who returned from a tutorial and the absence of their names from the compared copies. These proposed details must enter `setting.md` before that epigraph is published.

Open Measure extracts omit full bulletin headers for epigraph length, but a source handout should retain the conditions and actual sample record. Its numerical quantities are energy and time, which inhabitants can measure. The System knows no dice or resolution targets. The Surge excerpt is a compact statement of its energy cost; rounding remains a table instruction in the rules.

The humor belongs to human reactions, dry archival juxtaposition, and a researcher's impatience. System notices remain impersonal. The two visibility candidates now illustrate different limitations: translation is not verification, and classification can misread a sleeping subject.

The older PDF's Dying Scavenger outcome permits taking an unfinished core for VE, whereas the newer setting says interruption spoils it. Bestiary A follows the newer setting. Its associated encounter outcome should be reconciled before the epigraph is published.
