## Item 2: the six lore boxes

### 2.1 Cultivation: VE wearing the shape of what held it, and the green sweetness

**Placement:** `book/25-cultivation.md`, "Volatile Energy & Consolidation", after the Joe example and before "The Pressure Gauge". **Source voice:** prose, unattributed (the Principles boxes' form).

::: lore
Aether has a smell. Integrated humans describe green sweetness, fresh as leaves crushed between the palms and light enough to invite another breath, and on Earth people were calling it cut grass before anyone knew what it was. Each source adds an undertone that repeats for that source: a dry smoky finish, a warmer floral note, a musk, a low hum, a rhythm felt in the teeth. When something dies, the pale motes bring a brief wash of the sweetness and an uneven chord carrying the trace of what died, because the energy is still wearing the shape of the body that held it. Consolidation is that shape coming apart. The foreign notes fade, and the unevenness beneath the breastbone settles into a hum that follows the breath. A character who has smelled a thing's Aether once may recognize a resemblance later, before knowing its cause.
:::

GM line after the box: A recognized trace is evidence to investigate. It grants no bonus, no range, and no sense stat; Perception separates faint details, and experience teaches recognition.

### 2.2 Breakthroughs: Istren

**Placement:** `book/30-breakthroughs.md`, "In-World Framing", after its two paragraphs. **Source voice:** prose, unattributed.

::: lore
On Oren, a basin polity survives because one cultivator broke through to A-Grade. Istren's Ascendance broke the basin's isolation and opened routes no one else could hold, and the people who live behind those routes owe their lives to it. Centuries on, the emergency authority Istren took has never lapsed. Some citizens love them. Others want the right to take risks Istren forbids. Istren is insightful, generous, and unable to let go, and the argument about who decides has outlived everyone who was there when the routes opened.
:::

### 2.3 Titles: the Iron Court

**Placement:** `book/40-titles.md`, "Bestowed Titles", after the Examples line. **Source voice:** prose, unattributed.

::: lore
The Iron Court recognizes contracts, arbitrates disputes, issues credentials, and enforces its rulings within the jurisdictions that participate in it, and cultivators serve it. Hand of the Iron Court records affiliation with the institution; the System grants the title and endorses nothing about the Court. The Court offers formal equality beside rules its most powerful clients helped write. Sen Vale, one arbitrator among many, honors the text of an agreement and can be persuaded to ask whether a client concealed a material fact.
:::

### 2.4 Quests: the Rill Closure

**Placement:** `book/55-quests.md`, "Mandates", after "Refusing a Mandate brands the character...". **Source voice:** prose, unattributed.

::: lore
The Rill Closure is the case the wider world cites. A documented, inhabited sector was scheduled for retirement, notice was given, and the evacuation window was published. Some inhabitants lacked transport. Some refused to abandon what they depended on. The retirement proceeded on schedule. A deadline in a Mandate does not make everyone able to comply, and the System does not treat that as decisive.
:::

## Item 3: the three vignettes, two candidates each

First person, one voice each, from the setting's in-world sources. Pick one per vignette, or write your own and Claude edits it against the setting file.

### 3.1 Aether felt for the first time

**Placement:** `book/25-cultivation.md`, opening of "Volatile Energy & Consolidation", before the Joe example (or Core Mechanics, "Aether", after its first paragraph). **Source voice:** the survivors' forum.

**Candidate A.**

::: lore
"Day 4. Nobody warned me it would be nice. I came out of the gate and the first thing was the smell, cut grass, like someone had mowed the whole city, and I stood in a car park breathing it while people were screaming two streets over. It's in me too. There's a pressure behind my sternum that answers when I reach for it, and I keep reaching for it the way you tongue a loose tooth. My hands don't shake anymore. I ran three kilometers to my sister's flat and I wasn't tired. I don't know what I am now. I know I slept nine hours and woke up wanting more of it."

*the survivors' forum*
:::

**Candidate B.**

::: lore
"For the record, since everyone is describing it differently: it is sweet, it is green, it comes in with the breath, and it does not go away when you stop noticing it. It is strongest near the gate and near anything that has just died. I stood next to the Rift for ten minutes and took my own pulse before and after, and I feel better than the numbers say I should. I am aware that 'feel better' is the problem."

*the survivors' forum*
:::

### 3.2 Aura Pressure

**Placement:** `book/10-core-mechanics.md`, "Aura Pressure", after the rules and before the rule on benevolent NPCs suppressing their aura. **Source voice:** Bel Sar's route accounts (places, distances, what a road costs; an undated extract is labeled undated).

**Candidate A.**

::: lore
"Two days north of Latchwater, a traveler came the other way. I did not see them arrive. I was counting way-stones, and then I was on my knees on the road with my pack still on and no memory of deciding to kneel. It was certainty rather than fear, the way you are certain of the ground: this person could end me, and my body had the knowledge before my thoughts did. They passed at a walk. They did not look at me. When I could stand, the road was the same road and I could not have told you what they wore. The forty-first stone. I set it down so the next traveler knows the stretch."

*Bel Sar, route accounts, undated*
:::

**Candidate B.**

::: lore
"The toll between Pellam's ruin and the ford is not paid in coin. Something lives in the gorge. I never saw it. My knees knew it, and my hands, and for the length of the gorge I walked with one thought where I usually carry many, and the thought was: quietly. Two of the drovers ahead of me sat down on the path and could not be made to stand until the gorge had bent behind us. Count it as a day lost, and carry water for the day."

*Bel Sar, route accounts, undated*
:::

### 3.3 The philosopher

**Placement:** `book/20-principles.md`, after "The Quiet Path" and before "Running the Track (GM Reference)" (or The System AI, after "Attention" in the voice section). **Source voice:** the survivors' forum. Both candidates show the System answering a direct question, which the voice section says is rare; the GM text beside the box should say so in one line.

**Candidate A.**

::: lore
"I taught philosophy of mind. On Day 5 I asked it, out loud, in an empty lecture hall, whether I am the same person who woke up on Day 1. I did not expect an answer. It gave one: 'Pattern continuity: 94 percent. Divergence attributable to accession: 4 percent. Remainder: ordinary.' Ordinary. Thirty years on personal identity, and the answer is a percentage and the word ordinary. I have not decided whether it answered the question or replaced it. I have decided that it measured something, and that it does not know what my question was."

*the survivors' forum*
:::

**Candidate B.**

::: lore
"Asked it whether anything I do matters. Got: 'Query: outside measured scope. Observation continues.' Asked whether it matters to it. Got: 'Affirmative. Pattern retained.' I put both to what is left of my old department by email, and the two who still answer split the way they always split: one says it dodged, one says it is the most honest answer anyone has ever given me. I keep rereading the second message. It said it is keeping me. That is the sentence I cannot put down."

*the survivors' forum*
:::
