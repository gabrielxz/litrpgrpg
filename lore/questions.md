# The Lore Pass: Open Questions

*The worksheet for whatever the setting still lacks. Answered questions have moved into `lore/setting.md`, the setting's source of truth; this file holds only what is still open, plus the delivery list. Answer under each question in one-line facts and Claude moves them into the setting file.*

## How to answer

- **One line per fact.** A fact the app or a chapter can quote. Prose comes later, from the facts.
- **Tag a fact when it is not public:** `[GM]` (true, discoverable in play), `[nobody]` (deliberately unresolved). Untagged facts are what any Integrated adult knows.
- **Few and deep.** Three to five of anything.
- **Every System quirk passes the test:** it follows from something true about what the System is, and that truth is discoverable in play.
- **Name check.** Every coined name is checked against the rules vocabulary (Grade, Beat, Zone, Sector, Domain, Principle, Mandate, Trial, Accord, Aura) and against the other names for sound-alikes.
