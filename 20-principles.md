# The Principle System

## What a Principle Is

Principle is the fundamental fabric of reality: compressed patterns of how things behave, discovered and formalized by the System. A Principle is an operational truth: Fire consumes and spreads. Sharpness concentrates force into a decisive boundary. Space defines distance and adjacency. A character who internalizes such a truth can apply it.

Every Principle is organized around a **Concept**, the pattern's name and identity. Concepts range from the elemental (Fire, Earth, Lightning) to the abstract (Edge, Flow, Pressure, Consumption, Resonance). All valid Concepts share three properties:

- **Operational:** the Concept does something specific.
- **Bounded:** it does not apply everywhere.
- **Testable:** its use produces observable outcomes.

If a proposed Concept cannot meet all three, it is a mood, and the System does not compress moods. The GM enforces this at Compression (below).

**What a Principle grants.** A Principle does not raise stats. Across its tiers it grants:

- **Applications:** active techniques, in combat and out, costing Beats and Aether (Searing Strike, Spatial Step).
- **Passives:** resistances and affinities aligned to the Concept.
- **Infusion:** at Mid Fragment, the Concept woven into your ordinary actions at no Beat or Aether cost.
- **A Domain:** at Peak Fragment, a localized zone of systemic control.
- **Attunements:** things you simply can do: no roll, no cost.

## How the Track Works

Everything in this chapter is one loop. You live hard; the System hands you the memory of it; you meditate on that memory during rest; understanding accumulates as points; and when the points cross a threshold, you sit down, put the pattern into words, and the System grants power shaped like your answer.

```
   fight, risk, survive
            |
            v
    Battle Memory Card          (also: affinity treasures, visions,
            |                    surviving through the Concept)
            |   meditate during Consolidation
            v
    Insight Points (IP)
            |
            |   cross a threshold (3 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 100)
            v
        COMPRESSION             declare it during a Consolidation,
            |                   articulate the pattern
            v
    the tier's grant            Application, Infusion, or Domain
```

Principles advance on their own track. Levels and Grades measure how much power the System has integrated into your body; the Principle track measures how much of reality you understand. The two feed on the same dangerous life, so they usually rise together, but nothing forces them to. The track touches your Grade at exactly one point: the Domain gate.

**The terms, side by side:**

| **Term** | **One line** |
|---|---|
| Principle | A compressed truth about how reality behaves. |
| Concept | The truth's name and identity (Fire, Weight, Edge); what sits in a slot. |
| Concept Slot | Capacity to hold a Concept: one at F-Grade, a second at E-Grade, never more. |
| Insight Points (IP) | Accumulated understanding of one Concept; a visible running total. |
| Battle Memory | A card the GM grants for a moment of extreme stress; meditating on it during Consolidation yields IP. |
| Tier | A Concept's depth: Initial Insight, Seed, Early / Mid / Peak Fragment. |
| Compression | The Consolidation ritual that turns a met IP threshold into the next tier: articulate the pattern, receive the expression. |
| Application | An active technique granted by a tier (1 Beat plus Aether). |
| Application Grade | The Grade an Application was acquired at; fixes its Aether cost and scale forever. |
| Scale | How much world an Application's manifestation can touch; grows with Application Grade. |
| Infusion | The Mid Fragment grant: the Concept rides your ordinary actions for free. |
| Domain | The Peak Fragment grant: a zone where your Concept is briefly law. Requires a D-Grade vessel. |
| Attunements | A Concept's free capabilities: sensing it, handling it, living in it. |
| Refinement | A same-tier articulation that steers a Concept's identity (Fire into Consuming Flame). |
| Broadening | A tier-up articulation that widens the Concept itself (Weight into Gravity); part of Compression. |
| Re-forging | Rebuilding one known Application at your current Grade: new cost, new scale, old version gone. |
| Fusion | Merging two Concepts into one, freeing a slot. |

## Concept Slots

A character holds **one Concept** at F-Grade. Breaking through to E-Grade unlocks a **second slot**. Two is the lifetime maximum; no later Grade adds a third.

A slot, once filled, holds its Concept for life. The Concept can change shape (Refinement), widen (Broadening), or merge with the other (Fusion); it never simply drops away. The only way a slot opens again is Fusion: two Concepts become one, and the freed slot may later take a new Concept, which enters at the bottom like any other.

## Insight Points

Progression within a Concept is measured in **Insight Points (IP)**. IP totals are visible; the System reports them the way it reports levels:

::: systemvoice
**[Insight Gained: Weight 8/10]**
*The mountain does not strike. It arrives.*
:::

The character sees the number. What the System is forging from the number, it keeps to itself.

**Earning IP.** IP accrues only from experiences aligned with the Concept, and nearly all of it is earned under pressure. A character cannot grind generic experience into Sharpness insight: they must cut, be cut, study cutting, survive the edge.

| **Source** | **IP Awarded** |
|---|---|
| Battle Memory meditation | 1–3, by the memory's intensity |
| Consuming an affinity treasure | 1–2 |
| Consolidation vision | 1 |
| Surviving a life-or-death situation through the Concept | 2 |

**Consolidation visions are rationed.** A vision arrives only at a Consolidation that follows meaningful Concept-aligned experience, and never more than once per session. Rest alone produces nothing to see.

**A safe career earns nothing here.** Levels come from Volatile Energy, and VE comes from any kill; a cautious hunter who never takes a real risk still levels. The Principle track pays differently. No stress means no Battle Memories; no danger means no life-or-death insight. A season of careful hunting banks levels and not one point of Insight, while a single desperate hour can be worth a tier.

::: lore
Slower roads exist. The System honors comprehension however it arrives, and the Multiverse holds contemplatives who walked far up the ladder without drawing blood: monks who sat with Weight for sixty years until the mountain moved for them. Integration-era subjects rarely have sixty years. Pressure is the shortcut the System offers instead.
:::

## Battle Memories

**Battle Memories** are the primary pipeline from play to insight. In moments of extreme stress (surviving at near-zero HP, witnessing something beyond comprehension, achieving an outcome the System deems statistically improbable), the GM grants a **Battle Memory Card**. Two triggers are automatic: a Volatility cascade of two or more extra dice on a player character's roll, and surviving being Downed (see Core Mechanics, "Downed and Death").

During the next Consolidation, the player describes how their character meditates on the memory: what they felt, what they noticed, what pattern they think they glimpsed. The System returns a cryptic vision and awards IP toward the Concept the memory most closely expresses, 1 to 3 by the memory's intensity. The vision procedure for every run mode is in The System AI chapter; the unplugged version is three images composed by the GM.

**In play.** Kara (the brawler whose progression appears below) survived a cave-in by catching a falling slab and holding it while her partner dragged himself clear. The GM handed her a Battle Memory Card. At the party's next Consolidation:

**GM:** You're cycling, and the slab comes back. The full weight of it, on you again. What do you notice this time that you didn't in the moment?

**Kara:** In the moment I thought I was pushing up. But that's wrong. Nobody pushes up against a hillside. I think I was deciding where it would fall. It was always coming down; I picked "not yet, and not here."

**GM:** The memory holds still, like something is listening. Say what the pattern is.

**Kara:** Heavy things don't argue. They arrive. You can't stop something that heavy, but maybe you can be the one who says where it lands.

::: systemvoice
*A mountain hangs from a thread. The thread does not strain. Below, something waits to be told where to fall.*

**[Insight Gained: Weight 7/10]**
:::

That is the whole procedure. The player reaches for the pattern out loud, the System answers sideways, the points land. The reaching matters more than the polish: the GM should ask one or two questions and let the table sit with the answer. A vision hints and never teaches; if the player decodes it correctly, the payoff arrives in play, later.

## The Progression Ladder

| **Tier** | **Cumulative IP** | **Grants** |
|---|---|---|
| Initial Insight | 3 | Minor passive (e.g., +5% Fire Resistance) |
| Seed | 10 | First Application; Attunements |
| Early Fragment | 25 | Second Application; passive doubles |
| Mid Fragment | 50 | Infusion |
| Peak Fragment | 100 | Domain |

**The Domain gate.** A Domain requires a **D-Grade vessel**. The insight can be complete at any Grade: a character may hold 100 IP and a fully formed Peak Fragment understanding while still E-Grade. The body is the limit; holding open a zone of law takes a frame rebuilt twice by Breakthrough. IP accrues normally past 100, and the Domain Compression waits for D-Grade. This is the Principle track's one hard tie to Grade.

**What the economy delivers in practice.** With IP gated behind pressure, a character who lives dangerously reaches Seed midway through F-Grade, works through the Fragment tiers across E-Grade and into D, and Compresses a Domain at D-Grade. Nothing enforces that schedule; it bends toward the life lived. A cautious character reaches Seed late or never. A character the System keeps nearly killing runs ahead of it.

**Tiers beyond Peak Fragment** exist at C-Grade and above. This book does not cover them; a future supplement will.

## Compression

Meeting an IP threshold does not advance the tier. Advancement requires **Compression**, a declared act during Consolidation. The procedure:

1. **Declare.** During any Consolidation with a threshold met, the player declares Compression.
2. **The question.** The GM asks: *"What pattern have you discovered in how you act, or in how the world behaves?"*
3. **The answer.** The player answers from their character's lived experience: the fights, the Battle Memories, the moments that produced this IP.
4. **Refine together.** The GM (with the System AI, in assisted modes) works the answer with the player until it is Operational, Bounded, and Testable. This is a table conversation, and the GM carries as much of it as the player needs. A halting answer and a fluent one end in the same place: what is being compressed is the character's lived pattern, and the player's delivery has no effect on the result.
5. **The grant.** The System expresses the new tier: the Application, Infusion, or Domain, shaped by the articulation.

**The articulation can broaden the Concept.** At a tier-up, the re-articulation sometimes outgrows the Concept's name, and the System recognizes the larger truth. Weight, articulated again at Fragment depth ("everything falls toward something, and I choose the direction"), can become **Gravity**; Gravity, lifetimes deeper, might become **Dominion**. Broadening is a possibility inside the articulation, never a separate procedure and never owed: most tier-ups deepen the Concept under its own name. Existing Applications keep their names, costs, and scales; the new tier's grant takes the broadened identity.

**Refinement.** At any Compression, the articulation can instead steer the Concept at its current tier. A character whose Fire has grown hungrier with every fight may Compress Fire into **Consuming Flame**: same slot, same tier, same IP, shifted identity. The System adjusts the Concept's Applications and Attunements to match the new reading. Refinement grants no new tier; what it buys is a Concept that keeps up with who its holder is becoming, which the Hidden Vector Engine is tracking either way.

**New Concepts enter at the bottom.** Mastery is per-Concept and never transfers. Whether the second slot opens at E-Grade or Fusion frees a slot late in a career, a new Concept starts at 0 IP and climbs to Initial Insight and Seed like any novice's. Two rules keep late acquisition honest:

- The new Concept's Applications are priced and scaled at their **acquisition Grade** (see "Application Grade" below). A Seed Application acquired at C-Grade costs 10,000 Aether and manifests at C-Grade scale.
- Its IP must come from experiences aligned to the *new* Concept: attention and risk spent there instead of deepening what the character already holds.

The result: a Concept acquired at C-Grade has a beginner's toolkit, one Application, swung with a titan's arm. It knows one trick, and the trick is enormous.

**Example progression:** Kara, an F-Grade brawler, fights heavy and direct. Over six sessions she banks two Battle Memories (2 IP, 3 IP), survives a cave-in by holding a falling slab (2 IP), and receives one Consolidation vision (1 IP): 8 IP toward **Weight**. Two sessions later a third Battle Memory (2 IP) carries her to 10. At her next Consolidation she Compresses, articulates the pattern ("things fall the way I decide"), and the System grants her Seed Application: **Sudden Weight** (1 Beat, 10 Aether): her strike lands with the mass of something far larger, +10 to the Clash, and at F-Grade scale it can stagger a grown man, buckle a door, crack floorboards. She is Level 6 with POW 18; the 10-Aether cost is heavy but castable, exactly once per fight with room to spare.

## Activation Costs

| **Tier** | **Beat Cost** | **F origin** | **E origin** | **D origin** | **C origin** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seed Application | 1 Beat | 10 | 100 | 1,000 | 10,000 |
| Early Fragment Application | 1 Beat | 15 | 150 | 1,500 | 15,000 |
| Mid Fragment Infusion | Free | Free | Free | Free | Free |
| Peak Fragment Domain | 1 Beat to activate | not possible | not possible | 3,000 + 500/round | 30,000 + 5,000/round |

A cost is set once, at the moment the Application is acquired, and never changes (see Core Mechanics, "Aether Costs Are Set at Acquisition"). Costs scale ×10 per Grade of origin. The Domain row starts at D because Domains require a D-Grade vessel; there is no F-origin or E-origin Domain.

**What the Aether buys.** Every Seed Application grants a bonus inside the Modifier Budget, around +10, at every Grade; the table math never inflates. What scales is the technique's **scale**: an F-Grade flame marks flesh, a D-Grade flame opens a steel door. The Aether price is the price of scale. When an E-Grade character pays 100 for a fresh Seed Application, they are buying an E-scale manifestation, and the +10 rides along unchanged. The full treatment is under "Application Grade" below.

**Principle and Spells:** A character's Principle passives always apply to matching spells automatically. Using an active Application alongside a spell requires Infusion tier. Below that, choose one per Beat: cast the spell or use the Application.

## Application Grade

Every Application has a Grade of its own, set at the moment it is acquired. That Grade fixes two things for the Application's whole existence: its Aether cost, and its **scale**: how much world the manifestation can touch. The Clash bonus stays inside the Modifier Budget at every Grade; what grows is everything around the number.

**Searing Strike at three acquisition Grades** (the Seed Application of a Fire Concept):

- **F-Grade (10 Aether):** +10 to a Clash against targets vulnerable to fire. The flame wraps the weapon's edge, ignites cloth and dry wood, and leaves scorch lines on flesh.
- **E-Grade (100 Aether):** the same +10. The flame runs white and dense: it chars through leather, ruins the temper of a parried blade, and the wounds it leaves cauterize shut, still smoking.
- **D-Grade (1,000 Aether):** the same +10. The strike arrives as a sheet of fire with an edge in it. It passes through a steel door as through bread, and the Zone smells of ozone for hours.

Scale is fictional permission, priced by Grade. When a use collides with the world (can it burn this? can that survive it?), the GM reads the Application's Grade against the obstacle's Grade the same way any Cross-Grade question resolves.

**Re-forging.** Re-forging replaces an Application: the old version is gone the moment the new one exists. During any Compression, a character may re-forge one Application they already know at their current Grade; it takes the new Grade's cost and scale. An F-forged Searing Strike stays 10 Aether forever, a lit match that a D-Grade warrior can flick all day. Re-forged at D-Grade it becomes a door-cutter that costs 1,000 a swing, and the lit match no longer exists. Keeping the cheap old trick or paying full price for real power is a genuine choice, made one Application at a time.

## Attunements

From Seed tier, a Concept grants **Attunements**: capabilities that need no roll, Beat, or Aether. Baseline for every Concept:

- You **sense** the Concept's presence and behavior nearby (flame, edges, spatial distortion).
- **Mundane interactions** with the Concept stop requiring rolls (lighting a fire, splitting kindling clean, judging a distance exactly).
- You are **comfortable in environments** the Concept dominates (extreme heat, knife-storms of debris, warped space).

Specific Attunements beyond the baseline are generated by the GM and System AI to fit the Concept, and they deepen as tiers rise. Attunements are fictional capability rather than combat mechanics: if a use would demand a Clash roll, it is an Application.

## Principle Fusion

A character holding **two Concepts at Seed tier or higher** may attempt to fuse them during a Compression. With two slots for life, this is the largest commitment the track offers: both identities go in, one comes out.

Fusion is a Resistance Roll:

> **d100 + PER Force vs. Severe (140) of the character's Grade**

- **Success:** the two Concepts merge into a single fused Concept (Earth + Fire become **Magma**, Edge + Flow become **Severance**) at the **lower parent's tier**, occupying **one slot**. The System AI generates bespoke Applications for it. The freed slot is empty and may later take a new Concept, entering at the bottom like any other.
- **Failure:** the patterns refuse unification. The higher-tier parent loses 10 IP, and the fusion cannot be reattempted until after the character's next Breakthrough.

Fused Concepts are more powerful and less stable than their parents: their Applications price at the peak of the Modifier Budget (+15 to +20), and any natural 01–05 rolled while using one resolves as a Catastrophic Failure.

## At the Table in Each Run Mode

Everything on this track that says "the System returns" or "the System AI generates" has a manual answer.

- **Unplugged:** the GM composes Battle Memory visions from the three-image procedure in The System AI chapter, refines Compression articulations against Operational, Bounded, and Testable by hand, and invents Attunements and Application effects priced against the Modifier Budget.
- **AI-Assisted:** between sessions, paste the standing campaign context and the relevant function prompt (The System AI chapter) and treat the output as a draft. Visions tolerate a week's delay; Compression happens live at the table, so run it as the Unplugged procedure and let the AI polish the resulting Application afterward.
- **Companion App:** the app drafts visions and Applications from the session transcript; the GM reviews before anything reaches a player.

## Design Intent

Principles are discovered in play rather than chosen from a list, because the discovery *is* the behavioral signal. What a character does under pressure, recorded by the Hidden Vector Engine, determines which Concepts the System offers, which Battle Memories resonate, and what a Compression articulation can honestly claim. The axis-by-axis Concept affinities are documented in the Hidden Vector Engine chapter.

Slots are scarce and entry is always at the bottom so that a character's one or two Concepts, their Refinements, and the Fusion that may one day join them read as the mechanical transcript of who that character has been. The track runs beside the level track rather than inside it: the System integrates power on its own schedule, and hands out understanding only when it has been lived.

## The System and Principles

::: lore
The System exists to discover and refine stable patterns of reality that cannot be derived directly. Reality is not fixed: zones shift, laws degrade, unknown conditions emerge faster than any static model can solve. So the System introduces conscious agents into constrained environments and observes: conflict reveals limits, scarcity reveals priorities, uncertainty reveals models, pressure reveals identity.

Patterns that succeed across contexts are compressed, formalized, and made reusable. That is a Principle, and that is why the System pressures its holders: dominant strategies are challenged, over-reliance is punished, and no single approach remains optimal forever. The System does not ask "Did you win?" It asks "How do you win, and what does that reveal?"

Power is not granted. It is recognized, compressed, and returned to those who discovered it.
:::
