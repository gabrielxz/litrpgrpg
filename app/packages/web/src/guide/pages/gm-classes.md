# Classes

Classes is where you write the three class offers a character is due at Level 10, record a choice for a player who is away, and track the classes held. The rules are in Classes, "Building a Class for a Specific Human".

![The Classes section: a character's record at the left and the three offers being written beside it.](gm-classes.webp)

The number beside **Classes** in the section bar counts the living characters at Level 10 or higher with no class and no offers standing. Until someone reaches Level 10, the section says so and shows nothing else.

## Writing the three offers

A character who is due gets a panel titled **Kara is due three class offers**. At the left is **Kara's record**: Attributes, Background, the Deep leads, the circled moments from the HVE sweeps, the Principle or Insight, Proficiencies, and active titles.

To the right are three editors, **Offer 1** to **Offer 3**. For each:

1. **Start from** a book class or **Written fresh**. The list marks the classes whose two poles both lead the character's Deep ("fits the Deep leads") and the guarded ones. Picking a book class fills every field; edit from there.
2. **Name** and **The System's notice**: the in-world text the player reads, in the System's units. A warning appears if it uses the table's words.
3. **Profile**: Fixed, Guided, or Open, with the assigned points it places (3, 2, or 1). The line beside it shows how many are assigned and how many return to the player each level. The first Attribute is the **Lead**; **Add an Attribute** adds up to three.
4. **Technique**, its **Cost**, and **What it does**. The checkboxes mark a technique that changes the action economy, takes no Beat of its own, or is used on someone else's turn. A Drawback technique asks which drawback it costs, or leaves the choice to the player each time.
5. **The app applies**: nothing, a bonus to a Clash, or Health restored. With a hook, the app adds the bonus or applies the heal when the technique is used; without one, the app pays the cost and you apply the effect.
6. **Permission** and **What it permits**. **The app runs** picks one of the shapes the app handles in a fight (a Rush, a free move or Disengage, a reaction, cover, a Surge paid in Health, and others), or nothing, leaving it to the table. Mark it **Once a day, ready again at dawn** where it applies.
7. **Carries a guarded power (known to the GM only)** for a class from the guarded list. The player never sees the flag. A warning appears when more than one guarded class would be in play in the campaign.

Problems that stop the offers from recording (a missing notice, a profile with the wrong number of points, two offers with one name) show above the button. Warnings do not block.

The editors keep your work in this browser until you record, so you can leave and come back.

When the three are ready, check the preview and press **Offer Kara these three classes**. The player receives the classification notice with the three offers, and the cards appear on their screen.

### Drafting with the AI

With the campaign's AI key set, **Draft the three offers from the record** opens above the editors. Write **What the player keeps doing that no rule asked for**, check **Ask for a guarded power** if you want one offer to carry one, and press **Draft three offers**. The draft runs in the background.

When it lands, each offer is listed with its role and what it weighs. **Load them into the three offers** fills the editors; **Dismiss** drops the draft. Nothing reaches the player until you press **Offer Kara these three classes**.

## Offers standing

After you record, the panel becomes **Kara's offers stand**. The offers wait on the player's choice, and the assigned points of Level 10 and later wait with them. The player normally accepts one on their own screen. For a player away, press **Record that Kara accepts Breaching Vanguard** under the chosen offer.

To write different offers, undo the offer in the [Campaign log](guide:gm-log); the character is due again.

## Classes held

**Classes held** lists each class in the table's words. Inspection never shows a class; only its holder's player sees it.

- If the selection bonus pushed the lead Attribute past the stat cap, the card says how much was lost.
- A technique used outside a fight can be recorded here: **Record Joe's Triage**, with the cost in the label, pays the cost and, for a heal, restores the Health to the character you pick: the user or someone in their party. While the character is in a fight, it is used from the [Combat](guide:gm-combat) tracker instead. A Clash-bonus technique and a once-per-fight technique are used only in a fight.
- A once-a-day permission shows "ready" or "used since dawn" against dawn on the in-game clock, with **Record Kara's use of Finder's Share** while it is ready. The player can mark the use on their own screen as well. Without the clock set, the card asks you to keep the count yourself.
