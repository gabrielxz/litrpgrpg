# Fights and dice

When the GM starts a fight, it appears at the top of the At the table tray, and you act for your own character from there. The dice below it are for any other roll the GM calls for. The rules are in Core Mechanics, "Combat".

![The At the table tray during a fight: the sides in turn order, each character's Beats, and the Clash under way, with Dice and Ask the rules folded.](player-fight.webp)

The server rolls every die. Whatever you press in a fight records at once, and the GM and the other players see the result. If you press something by mistake, tell your GM, who can undo it. The GM records anything you do not.

## Reading the fight

The fight shows its name and the round. Below are the sides in turn order:

- **Momentum** marks the side that holds it; **Turn** marks the side acting now. A line warns when Momentum will shift next round.
- Each combatant shows tags for **Exposed**, **Downed**, **Suppressed**, and **Surprise**, and the Zone they stand in when the fight has more than one.
- A player character shows its Beats as pips. You see no creature's Health unless your class reads it.
- Under the sides, a line sums up the last Clash: who hit whom and for how much, or who turned the attack.

## Your turn

When your side has the turn and nobody else is acting, **Act** appears beside your character. Press it to take your activation. The panel then shows how many Beats you have left and what you can spend them on.

- **Attack…** opens the attack form (below).
- Pick a Zone and press **Move (1 Beat)**.
- **Pill…** gives a pill you carry to yourself or someone in your Zone. The form warns when the target has taken 5 of that kind since their last Consolidation, after which a pill does nothing.
- **Stabilize…** works on a Downed ally in your Zone: a Moderate Dexterity check. Check **Medical Background (Advantage)** if yours covers it.
- **Execute…** kills a Downed combatant: 1 Beat, no roll.
- Your class technique, if it is used on its own, appears by name. A heal asks whom it restores; any other effect is applied by the GM once the cost is paid.
- **Push back (1 Beat)** when you are Suppressed, or **Intervene…** for a Suppressed ally: another Will Save.
- **Done** ends your activation.

A class permission that moves you or disengages you for no Beat adds its own button, named for the permission.

## Attacking

1. Pick the **Target**. **What (optional)** names the attack, such as Axe.
2. **Attack with**: STR for heavy melee, DEX for finesse or ranged, POW for spells. Each shows your Force.
3. **With** a weapon shape adds your Proficiency bonus in it. An exploding roll earns a Mark in that shape.
4. Add **Modifiers**, check **Advantage** if the GM granted it, and **Surge** (+5 for its Aether cost, declared before the roll). A technique that joins a Clash shows as a checkbox with its bonus and cost.
5. **Flanking +10** is checked for you when another enemy of the target shares its Zone. Change it if the table sees it differently.
6. Press **Kara attacks (1 Beat)**.

The defender then rolls. When a creature defends, the GM rolls for it.

## Defending and Yield

When someone attacks your character, the tray shows who. Choose **Defend with** (DEX to dodge, FOR to absorb, HRT against the mind and spirit, PER against illusion), the shape, modifiers, Advantage, and Surge, and press **Roll the Clash**.

If you lose, the tray shows both totals and the Margin, and one button per choice: **Take 40**, **Yield 1 Beat: 20**, and so on. Each Beat you Yield takes 20 off the Margin and comes out of your next turn. A button says when you would be Driven Back. If an ally can still cut the Margin, the tray says so; wait for them before you choose. The rules are in Core Mechanics, "Yield".

## Off your turn

A class reaction or cover shows as its own button when it applies, such as a strike for no Beat, or a cut to an ally's Margin paid from your next turn.

## Spoils

After a fight, loot the party has not divided shows under **Spoils**. Pick who it is for and press **Take one**.

## Dice

Open **Dice** in the tray for any roll outside a fight.

1. Choose **Clash** or **Check**. If you play more than one character, pick **Who rolls**.
2. Pick the **Force**, add **Modifiers**, and check **Advantage** if granted. A Clash also offers **With** (a weapon shape) and **Surge**.
3. **What for (optional)** labels the roll.
4. Press **Roll for Kara**.

The list below shows the newest 40 rolls at the table, everyone's except the GM's private ones: the dice, the bonus, the total, and **exploded** when a die exploded. Clashes from the fight appear here too. The GM reads success or failure against the Resistance; you see the total.
