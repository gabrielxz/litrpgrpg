# Building a character

You build your character on the registration form: your own from a point buy, or one of the book's ready-made characters. The rules are in Character Creation.

![The registration form with Build your own selected: name, Background, and the seven Attributes.](player-creator.webp)

## Where to start

- **In a campaign.** When you open a campaign with no character of yours in it, your screen reads *Interface: awaiting registration.* Press **Build a character**. Your GM may make one for you instead; if they do, it appears on your screen with nothing for you to press.
- **Before you have a campaign.** You can build a character from Home and keep it until you join one. See [Getting started](guide:start).

## Build your own

Choose **Build your own** at the top of the form.

1. Type a **Name**.
2. Write the **Background**: one or two lines of life before Integration, such as an ER nurse of twelve years or a land surveyor. What a Background does in play is in Core Mechanics, "Backgrounds".
3. Spend the Attributes. You have 40 points across the seven Attributes, each between 3 and 10. They start at 5 each, so 5 points are left to place. Use **−** and **+** beside each one. The heading shows how many points are left, or how many you are over.

Beside each score is what that score means in the world, from the book's anchors, so you can see what a 3 or a 9 in Perception looks like. Under the Attributes, the form shows the Health (Fortitude × 2) and Aether (Power) the scores give you.

The line above the button says what still stops the form: a missing name, a missing Background, or points not spent to exactly 40. When nothing does, the button is ready.

## Play a ready-made character

Choose **Play a ready-made character** to pick one of the book's characters: Kara, a warehouse shift lead; Joe, a volunteer firefighter; or Andre, a land surveyor. Each card shows the character's line and Attributes. Pick one.

## Register

Press **Register**. The character is recorded at once and your interface appears in place of the form, at Level 1 and F-Grade. **Cancel** leaves the form without building anything.

If you ever need to change something you registered, ask your GM, who can correct or undo it.

## Bringing a character you built

If you built characters from Home that are not in a campaign yet, the waiting screen lists them under **Or bring one you built**. Press **Bring Kara** to move that character into this campaign. A character belongs to one campaign; once brought in, it leaves your Home list for good.

## After registration

What happens next comes from play: the GM awards Volatile Energy, you level, and you place your free points. See [Your screen](guide:player-screen) and [Moments](guide:player-moments).
