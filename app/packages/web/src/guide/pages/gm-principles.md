# Principles

Principles is where you run each character's Principle track: the Battle Memory Cards the rules make due, cards granted from the fiction, meditations and Insight, naming the Principle a family crystallizes into, and Distillation. The rules are in The Principle System.

![The Principles section: cards due at the top, a card per character, and Grant a Battle Memory at the side.](gm-principles.webp)

The number beside **Principles** in the section bar counts what waits on you: cards due, families ready to be named, and Distillations available.

Every form previews what changes and the notice the player receives. What you record reaches the player when you press; undo it from the [Campaign log](guide:gm-log).

## Battle Memory Cards due

The record makes a card due in two cases, and lists each one at the top of the section (and in [Suggestions](guide:gm-suggestions)):

- a cascade of 2 or more extra dice on a player character's roll: a Clash, a check, Momentum, or a roll from the dice;
- a character who survived Downed, when the fight ends.

Write **What the card says** (the moment in a line, up to 300 characters) and press **Grant Kara the card**. A card due for surviving Downed also offers **Withhold it**, for a Downing that taught nothing. A cascade's card cannot be withheld.

The player receives *Battle Memory retained.* and sees the card's text on their interface.

## Grant a Battle Memory

The side panel grants a card from the fiction. Pick the **Character**, write **What the card says**, and press **Grant the card**.

## A character's card

Each character has a card. It shows, from the top:

- each Principle held: its name, family, and tier; Insight against the next tier's threshold; the minor passive; and each grant with its articulation. **Distillation available** appears when the Insight meets the next threshold. **offered, waiting on the player** appears while a Quiet Path offer is open.
- **Resonance** for each family with Insight that has not crystallized, against the 3 that crystallizes it.
- **Battle Memories held**, with **chosen** on the card the player picked to meditate on.
- **Award Insight**, folded.

A dead character's card shows the record and no controls.

### Meditation

A card can be meditated on once a Consolidation has been recorded since it arrived. Until then its line reads "meditation at a later Consolidation". After that, press **meditation** on the card's line.

1. **Family** starts at the family the character's Deep leads toward. Change it to fit what the player felt.
2. Pick the **Insight**, 1 to 3, by the memory's intensity.
3. Write **What the player said**.
4. Write **The System's vision**: three images, as the placeholder lists them. With the campaign's AI key set, **Draft the vision** writes it from the card, the family, and the player's words, and proposes the Insight. A warning appears if the draft or your edit uses the table's words.
5. Press **Record the meditation**.

The player receives the vision and the Insight. The vision stays on their interface under Visions.

### Award Insight

Open **Award Insight** for the other sources. Each **Source** shows its range: an affinity treasure 1 to 2, a Consolidation vision 1 (once a day, dawn to dawn), surviving through the Principle 2, any other aligned experience 1 to 3. Pick the **Family** and the **Insight** and press **Award 2 Insight**.

## Naming a Principle

When a family reaches 3 Insight and the character has a free slot, the card opens a naming form. It shows the circled moments from the HVE sweeps and the player's words from meditations in that family. Say the name to the player first; they may ask once for another reading.

Type the **Principle** (the plainer word) and an optional **Minor passive**, then press **Name it Weight**. The player receives *Initial Insight: Weight.*

## Distillation

Press **Distill** on a Principle marked **Distillation available**. The tiers open at 10, 25, 50, and 100 Insight; Peak Fragment also needs a D-Grade body. The form states the test the articulation must pass: Operational, Bounded, Testable.

With the AI key set, type what the player has said (optional) and press **Draft two readings**. Each reading gives an articulation, a second phrasing, the grant, and how it meets the test; **Use this reading** fills the form.

Fill in:

- **The articulation**, in the player's words;
- **Broadened name (optional)**, if the Principle's name widens;
- the grant, labeled by tier: **Application** for Seed and Early Fragment, **Infusion** for Mid Fragment, **Domain** for Peak Fragment;
- its **Effect**, within the Modifier Budget the placeholder states;
- at Seed, **Attunements beyond the baseline**.

Press **Distill to Seed**. The player receives *Distillation complete* with the name, tier, and grant.

### The Quiet Path

Check **The Quiet Path: offer it for the player to confirm** to put the articulation to the player instead. You write **Phrasing one** and an optional **Phrasing two**, and the button reads **Offer it**. The player sees *Articulation proposed* on their screen and confirms one phrasing or declines. The Principle shows **offered, waiting on the player** and offers no Distill or Reshape until they answer.

## Reshape

**Reshape** changes an existing Principle's identity in the same slot with the same Insight ("Changing an Existing Principle"). The form asks for the articulation and a **New identity**, and records with **Reshape**. The Quiet Path works here too.
