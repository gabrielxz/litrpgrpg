# Player view

Player view shows any player's screen exactly as that player sees it, so you can check what a notice, a quest, or a fight looks like from their side. It sits in the **Table** group of the GM's sections.

![Player view showing Kara's interface and the At the table tray, with the Viewing as bar above.](gm-player-view.webp)

## Choosing a player

The bar at the top reads **Viewing as**, with a list of the campaign's players. It opens on the first player; pick another to switch. Characters you hold as the GM have no player, so they do not appear here; their sheets are in [the Party section](guide:gm-party).

With no players in the campaign, the section reads *No players yet. Invite them from the Table.* Invite links are in [the Table section](guide:gm-table). A player who has joined without a character shows *Interface: awaiting registration.*

## What you see

The screen below the bar is the player's own: each of their characters' interfaces, their notices, and the **At the table** tray. It refreshes whenever anything is recorded, so a notice appears here the moment you record the action that sends it, and a level-up or a title plays its moment as it lands. [The player's screen](guide:player-screen) describes each part.

The server builds this view the same way it builds the player's, so it holds only what What Can Be Seen lets that player see. Nothing the player cannot see reaches this screen, including:

- HVE entries, and the axis pair of an HVE-Resonant title
- the Saturation band and assigned points still to place
- counts toward titles and their thresholds
- other characters' sheets, beyond the party frame
- held System messages, until you send them
- private rolls, and the Resistance, outcome, and Battle Memory flag of any roll

The rules for what each character sees are in What Can Be Seen.

## What you cannot do here

Nothing on this screen can be changed. The controls a player uses to act are gone: spending free points, choosing a class, distilling a Principle, wearing or hiding a title, answering a quest, claiming from the spoils, handing on or using an item, inviting to a party, and acting in a fight. The tray has no roll form and no **Ask the rules**; the recent open rolls still list. The capture strip for listening is not part of this view.

**Inspect** on an interface still works, since a look records nothing.

> To change anything a player sees, record it from your own sections: the Record panel, Combat, Quests, or wherever the action lives. The change shows here at once.

## When to use it

- After recording an award, a title, or a System message, to read the notice as the player reads it.
- During a fight, to see the player's fight panel and what it offers them.
- To check what a Hidden quest shows in a player's quest log at its current reveal.
- To confirm what one character reads of another under **Inspect**.
