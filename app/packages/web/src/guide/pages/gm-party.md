# Party

Party is the section the GM's screen opens on: a sheet for every character in the campaign, the titles and spoils waiting on you, and the Record panel beside them for every change you make to a sheet outside a fight.

![The Party section with four character sheets, the spoils below them, and the Record panel on the right.](gm-party.webp)

In a new campaign, the **Set up the table** checklist sits at the top of the section until its three steps are done (see [Setting up a campaign](guide:gm-setup)).

## The character sheets

Each character has one sheet, in the order the characters were made. The head shows the character's mark and name, who plays them (a player's name, or **GM** for a character you hold), and their Level and Grade.

Below the head, tags show what needs attention:

- **Downed** or **Dead**, and the sheet dims.
- **Assigned points due: Level 9**: a level landed and its assigned points wait for you to place them in [the Record panel](guide:gm-record) under **Assigned points**.
- **2 free held**: free points the player has not spent yet. The player spends them on their own screen.
- The class name, once the character holds one, and **Class offers standing** while three offers wait on the player's choice (see [Classes](guide:gm-classes)).
- **Party:** and the other members' names.
- A Saturation tag, **Mild Saturation −10** or heavier, with **collapse clock running** at Critical. Players never see their Saturation band.

Four bars follow: **HP**, **Aether**, **Stored** (Volatile Energy held against the character's Tolerance; the bar turns red past Tolerance), and **Refined** (refined VE toward the next level, with how much is still to go, or **cap** at the Level cap). The statline lists the seven Raw Attributes.

Lines appear under the statline when they apply: Attributes temporarily down from a collapse, **Proficiencies** (weapon shape, tier, and Marks), **Titles** (with a note for one that is released, negative, or waiting on the player's stat choice), **Carries** (what the character holds), and the Background.

The sheets are read-only. Every change goes through the Record panel, a fight in [Combat](guide:gm-combat), or another section. To hand a character to another player or take one over, use **Who plays whom** in [the Table section](guide:gm-table).

With no characters in the campaign, the section says so. Players build their own once they join (see [Getting started](guide:start)); you can make one under **New character** in the Record panel.

## Titles due

When a character's count reaches the trigger for a catalog title (the record counts kills, Consolidations, surviving Downed, and more; you tick the rest under **Counts**), the title appears on the **Titles due** card above the sheets, with its bonus. The card shows only while a title is due.

Below the list, a preview in the System's register shows what each player receives: *Title conferred: First Blood.* Press **Confer** to grant the title, or **Pass** to decline it. Either records at once, with no second step; undo a mistake from [the campaign log](guide:gm-log). A title you pass on does not come due again for that character. The catalog is in Titles, "The F-Grade Achievement Catalog".

## The spoils

**The spoils** card lists items the party holds in common and has not divided: loot from a fight's aftermath, a quest's reward sent there, or anything you give to the spoils from the **Items** tab. The card shows only while the spoils hold something.

Players claim items from the spoils on their own screens. For a player away from theirs, choose a character in the row and press **Give one**: one item moves to that character at once, and the player's notice reads *Item registered*. Dead characters are left out of the list.

## The Record panel

The panel on the right holds the forms for every change outside a fight: dice, VE, rests, points, HP and Aether, items, titles, parties, System messages, and new characters. Each form previews what it records and what each player receives before you press its button. [The Record panel](guide:gm-record) covers every tab.
