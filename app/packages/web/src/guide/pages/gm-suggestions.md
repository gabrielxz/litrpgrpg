# Suggestions

Suggestions collects everything waiting on you: grants the AI drafted from table talk, and what the record itself makes due. The number on the **Suggestions** tab counts both.

![The Suggestions section with drafted grants on the left and Due from the record on the right.](gm-suggestions.webp)

## From the table talk

The left column lists what drafting raised. Drafting runs from the Events section, from pasted talk or from what the listening heard (see [Events](guide:gm-events) and [Listening](guide:gm-listening)); moments and bookkeeping stay in Events, and the grants below come here. With nothing waiting, the column says so.

Every card shows the lines it cites, with who said them, and a **Drafted because:** line with the drafter's reason. Nothing on a card reaches a player until you record it.

### Titles

A catalog title the fiction earned, named with its bonus. Press **Confer on Kara** to grant it as drafted, or **Dismiss**.

### Battle Memory Cards

Edit **What the card says** (up to 300 characters), then press **Grant Joe the card**.

### Hidden Achievements

The card holds the title's **Name**, **Trigger**, **Attribute**, and **Bonus**, all editable. When the drafter saw another way to read the deed, an **Another reading** line explains it; **Take it** switches the Attribute to that reading. Press **Confer on Nia** to grant it. The book's procedure is in The System AI, "Hidden Achievements and Titles".

### Quests and Personal Opportunities

A drafted quest shows as its log entry: code, Grade, Difficulty, objective, reward, and time. A Personal Opportunity drafted at the sweep (see [HVE](guide:gm-hve)) adds the System's words to the player, in italics, and your note. **Open in Quests to issue** takes you to the Quests section with the issue form filled in; issuing it there accepts the draft. The System's words go to the player only if you open **Send the System's words to Andre** and press **Send to Andre**.

### Partial reveals

For a fully obscured Hidden quest the table has come close to, the drafter proposes the suggestive name the log shows. Edit **The name the log shows** and press **Reveal it to Kara**.

### Prep cues and class offers

A Prep cue means the table reached the moment a prepared item was written for. Press **Fire it from Prep** to go to [Prep](guide:gm-prep) and fire it there. Drafted class offers are edited and recorded in [Classes](guide:gm-classes); here you can only dismiss them.

### Warnings on a card

Two warnings can appear under text a player will read. **The table's words** lists words such as round or roll that the System does not say. **Names the sheet's sides** lists HVE sides (Hunger, Accord) the player should never see named. Both are warnings: you may record anyway.

## Accepting, dismissing, and restoring

The accept buttons use the same preview as every form: the section shows what the grant does and the notice each player receives, and records nothing until you press. Once recorded, the card moves to **Accepted** at the foot of the column. **Dismiss** moves a card to **Dismissed**, where **Restore** brings it back.

Drafting the same talk again raises nothing you have already seen: the same title for the same character, or the same kind of grant drawn from a line already cited, stays accepted, dismissed, or waiting as you left it.

> Undoing an accepted grant in the [Campaign log](guide:gm-log) returns its card here, marked, to accept again or dismiss.

## Due from the record

The right column needs no AI. It lists, each with a link to the section that settles it:

- assigned points waiting for a character with no class
- a Principle crystallizing, or one that can be distilled
- class offers due at Level 10
- a quest whose time limit on the in-game clock has passed
- a quest whose count is met and whose reward is unpaid
- a fight that ended with its kills and loot unrecorded

Below the list sit two cards. **Titles due** lists catalog titles whose count reached its trigger, with the notice each player will read. **Confer** and **Pass** record at once; undo either from the Campaign log. **Battle Memory Cards due** lists the cards the rules make due: write the card and press **Grant Kara the card**. A card due from a Downing also offers **Withhold it**, for a Downing that taught nothing. The rules for cards are in The Principle System, "Battle Memories".
