# Quests

Quests is where you issue System Quests and run each one to its end: progress, completion with VE and items, failure, or expiry. What a player's quest log shows follows from what you record here. The rules for categories, rewards, and refusals are in System Quests.

![The Quests section: the issue form, then the open quests as log entries.](gm-quests.webp)

## Issue a quest

The **Issue a quest** form sits at the top.

1. Pick the **Category**: Mandate, Personal Opportunity, Routine, Hidden, or Faction or Bestowed.
2. **Code** fills itself with the next free code (Q-101 onward, M-01 onward for Mandates). Type over it to choose your own.
3. Write the **Title** and **Objective**. A Faction quest also takes an **Issuer**.
4. Set **Grade** and **Difficulty**. The **VE** field's placeholder shows the book's reward for that category, Difficulty, and Grade; leave it empty to use that amount, or type another. **Proportional** leaves VE to be set at completion.
5. For a counted objective, give a **Count**. Sharing a quest multiplies the count by the holders; check **Stays as written when shared** for a place reached or a person protected.
6. Add a **Time** in words, and a **Limit (hours)** counted on the in-game clock from the moment of issue. The limit opens only while the clock is set (see [the table bar](guide:gm-session)).
7. Add reward items with **Add a reward item** (the catalog's names are offered as you type) and any **Other reward**.
8. A Personal Opportunity takes a **Flavor** (combat, social, exploration). A Hidden quest takes what **The log shows**: nothing readable, a suggestive name (typed under **Suggestive name**), or nothing until complete.
9. **GM note (never on their log)** is for you alone.
10. Pick the recipient. Routine, Faction, and Personal Opportunity quests are **Offered to:** one character; a Mandate or Hidden quest **Binds:** every character you check.
11. Check the preview, including the notice each player receives, and press **Issue [Q-101]**.

A warning appears if the title, objective, or reward uses the table's words (round, roll), which the System does not say.

To keep a quest for later, open **Save this quest to Prep instead** and press **Save to Prep**. A quest fired from [Prep](guide:gm-prep), or opened from a draft in [Suggestions](guide:gm-suggestions), arrives in this form filled in; issuing it fires the prepared item or accepts the draft.

## Running open quests

Open quests are listed as log entries: code, issuer, Grade and Difficulty, objective with its count, reward, time, due time on the in-game clock with the hours left, and status. Under each entry, who it is offered to or held by, whether it was shared with the party, and who refused it.

The buttons on a quest card record at once, and the players' notices go out when you press. Hover a button to read the notice first. Undo any of them from the [Campaign log](guide:gm-log).

- **Accept for Kara** and **Refuse for Kara** answer an offer for a player away from their screen. Players normally answer on their own screen.
- **+1** and **−1** move a counted objective.
- **Failed** fails the quest.
- **Expire** closes it silently: no refusal is counted and no notice is sent.
- **Partial reveal**, on an active fully obscured Hidden quest, gives it the suggestive name you type beside the button.

### Completing a quest

**Complete…** opens the completion panel.

1. Uncheck any holder who did not meaningfully take part.
2. Each holder's VE field shows the stated award. A holder above the quest's Grade shows 0. Type another amount to change it; for a Proportional quest, set each amount here.
3. If the quest carries items, choose where **Items go to**: the spoils or one holder.
4. Check the preview and press **Complete [Q-101]**.

You can complete a quest whose count is short.

> The record never fails or expires a quest on its own. When a time limit passes or a count is met with the reward unpaid, [Suggestions](guide:gm-suggestions) lists it under Due from the record until you settle it.

## Personal Opportunities refused

When a character has refused Personal Opportunities, a card lists their refusals by flavor. At 3 refusals of a flavor it notes "half as often", and at 6 "stops appearing". The consequences are in System Quests, "Refusal and Failure Consequences". Drafting an offer at the sweep is in [HVE](guide:gm-hve).

## Closed quests

Completed, failed, refused, and expired quests fold under a line at the foot of the section with their count. Open it to read them back.
