# Moments

Some notices mark a turn in your character's story: a level, a title, a new technique, a class, a Distillation, a quest, and being Downed. Your interface plays a short motion for each when it lands while your screen is open. If you open the screen later, you see the result without the motion.

## Level-ups

The GM awards Volatile Energy; when it carries you past the bar, you receive *Level 9 attained.* and the Level in your head rolls over.

Each level brings 5 points. The System assigns 3: the GM places them until you hold a class, and your class's profile places them after (a profile may hand some back to you). You receive *Attributes allocated* with where they went. The other 2 are yours. At E-Grade the counts are ten times larger, at D-Grade a hundred times. The rules are in Progression, "Leveling Up".

Your free points show on the Attributes as *Unallocated points: 2*.

1. Press **+** under an Attribute for each point you place there, **−** to take one back.
2. Press **Allocate**. The points land at once and you receive *Allocation recorded*.

You can keep points unplaced as long as you like; they wait for you.

## Class offers

At Level 10 the GM writes three classes for your character. You receive the classification notice, and a **Classification** section opens with three cards. Each shows the System's text, then its mechanics: the profile (how your assigned points grow), the technique, and the permission.

![Three class offers on a player's screen, each with the System's text and its mechanics.](player-class-offers.webp)

Press **Accept…** on the one you want. The card asks you to confirm, since the other two close; press **Accept Breaching Vanguard**, or **Not yet** to look again. The offers stay until you choose one, and you cannot refuse all three. Your assigned points from Level 10 wait until you do.

Once accepted, the **Class** section shows your class. You receive *Class accepted* and the bonus to its lead Attribute.

- A technique you can use outside a fight shows **Use Triage** with its cost. In a fight you use it from the tray.
- A once-a-day permission shows whether it is ready or used, and **Use** with its name. Press it when you use the permission; it is ready again at dawn.

Nobody else can see your class, by inspection or otherwise.

## Principles and Distillation

The **Principle** section opens when your first Battle Memory or Insight arrives.

- *Resonance accruing* shows a family's Insight against the 3 that makes it crystallize. When it does, the GM names it with you, and you receive *Initial Insight* with the name.
- **Battle Memories** lists the cards you hold. Press **Meditate on this** on the one you want to meditate on at your next Consolidation; **Set aside** undoes it. The GM records the meditation and the System's vision, which you keep under **Visions**.
- Your Principle shows its name, tier, Insight, passive, and what each Distillation granted.

The GM usually runs a Distillation with you at the table and records it. On the Quiet Path, the System proposes the articulation instead: you receive *Articulation proposed* and one or two phrasings appear under the Principle. Press **Confirm** beside the one that is true of your character, or **Decline**. The rules are in The Principle System, "Distillation" and "The Quiet Path".

## Inspect

The **Inspect** section lets your character look at another being: every other living player character, and everyone in a running fight. It costs nothing. Pick a name under **Look at…**.

- You read their titles that are visible to you.
- *does not resolve* means the being is a Grade or more above you.
- *no life reads* means the System reads them as dead.

Classes, hidden titles, and titles not worn never show. The rules are in What Can Be Seen, "What You See of Others".

## Downed

When your character drops to 0 Health in a fight, you receive *Vital coherence: 3. Falling. Stabilization: required.* The interface marks you as falling, and a bar under your name shows three segments that go out one at a time, with a notice for each. Your party sees you as Downed. You cannot act while Downed.

An ally stabilizes you or heals you; you receive *Vital coherence: stable.* When you come to, you receive *Consciousness restored* with your Health. If your character dies, the interface reads *Deceased.* The rules are in Core Mechanics, "Downed and Death".

Surviving Downed can earn a Battle Memory Card when the fight ends.
