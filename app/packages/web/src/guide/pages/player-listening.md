# Listening

Your GM can have the app listen to the table, so it can draft what happened for the GM to confirm. Listening needs your consent, and you control your own microphone from the strip at the top of your screen.

![The listening strip above a player's screen, with the consent buttons at the right.](player-listening.webp)

## The strip

The strip sits under the top bar. On the left it shows the table's state:

- **Not listening**: nothing is being captured.
- **Listening**: the table is listening.
- **Paused**: the GM paused listening for everyone.

Beside it, a line says what your own microphone is doing, such as "Your microphone is live.", "Your microphone is muted.", "Your microphone is off until the GM resumes.", "You are not at the table this session.", or "Another of your tabs has the microphone." While your microphone is live, a small level meter moves as you speak.

## Consenting

Press **Consent to listening…** to read what listening does before you agree. In short:

- while the GM has listening on during a session, your tab sends your microphone to the Gradebreaker server, which passes it to Soniox to turn into text;
- neither keeps the audio;
- the text appears on the GM's screen, where the app uses it to draft what happened, and is deleted 30 days after it was said;
- you can mute your tab at any time.

Press **I consent**, or **Not now** to close it. Your consent belongs to you in this campaign; only you can give or withdraw it.

The table listens only while everyone at it has consented. If you are at the session without consent, the GM cannot start listening, and the GM's screen shows that it is waiting on you. Nothing you say reaches another player.

## Your microphone

Your microphone opens only while all of these are true: the GM has listening on, you have consented, your character is marked at the table this session, and you have not muted.

- **Mute** stops your tab sending. **Unmute** starts it again. The GM sees you as muted.
- The first time, your browser asks for the microphone. If it cannot open one, the strip says why and offers **Try again**, or **Turn my microphone on** when the browser needs a press first.
- Only one of your tabs captures at a time, the newest. An older tab shows **Use this tab** to take the microphone back.

## Withdrawing

**Withdraw consent** on the strip withdraws it at once. If you are at the table, listening stops for everyone, and the GM's screen says who withdrew.

## Test recordings

Test recordings are separate from listening. They keep your voice so the listener can be measured against real speech. Press **Test recordings…** to read the terms:

- while the GM records a listening session for testing, the server saves what your microphone sends, with the text made from it, for the GM to download;
- it is deleted when the GM deletes it, or 7 days after the recording ends;
- your strip shows **Recording for testing** while your voice is being kept.

Press **I consent to test recordings** to agree. Without this consent your voice is never recorded, even when the GM records others. **Withdraw test-recording consent** deletes your part of every test recording still on the server.
