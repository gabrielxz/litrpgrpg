# Asking the rules

**Ask the rules** answers a rules question from the book, with the chapter and heading each part of the answer comes from. It sits in the At the table tray on your screen.

## Asking

1. Open **Ask the rules** in the tray. While a fight runs it is folded closed; press its heading to open it.
2. Type your question, such as `Can I Yield against a free strike?`
3. Press **Ask**, or Enter. Shift and Enter start a new line.

The button reads **Looking it up…** while the answer is written. The answer appears under the question, newest first, with the chapters and headings it cites, such as Core Mechanics, "Yield". Your last 10 answers stay on the screen until you leave the page.

## What it reads

Your answers come from the chapters players read: Introduction, Core Mechanics, Character Creation, Progression, Classes, The Principle System, Cultivation, Grade Breakthroughs, The Hidden Vector Engine, What Can Be Seen, and Quick Reference. The GM's version reads every chapter.

It reads nothing from your campaign: your character, the fight, and everything the GM has recorded stay out of it, so an answer cannot tell you anything your character does not already know. Ask it about rules; ask your GM about your situation.

When those chapters do not answer the question, the answer says so and adds "Not answered in the players' chapters: the GM's call." A question about your table's story gets the rule, with the rest left to the GM.

## Who sees it

Nobody else sees your questions or the answers, and nothing is recorded in the campaign.

> The answer is a reading of the book. At the table, the GM's ruling stands.

## When it does not work

Asking uses the campaign's AI key, which the GM sets. Without one, the tray says so in place of the field. Ask your GM to set it, or look the rule up in the book.
