# Bestiary

The Bestiary section is your reference for the book's creatures: every stat block, and the encounter sizing table read for the party you have. Nothing here records anything; you build fights under [Combat](guide:gm-combat).

![The Bestiary with stat blocks on the left and the encounter sizing table on the right.](gm-bestiary.webp)

## Stat blocks

The left column lists every creature in the book's Bestiary chapter. The **Tier** list at the top narrows it to one group: **Trash**, **Peer**, **Elite**, or **Boss**. **All** shows every block.

Each block shows:

- The creature's name, an *Imprint* tag where it applies, and its Grade and tier.
- **HP**, **Beats**, **HRT**, and **PER**, and **Aether** for a creature that has it.
- **Off** and **Def**: each attack and defense as a two-digit Force, its stat, and what it is (*06 DEX (bite)*).
- *Yields* or *Does not Yield*. A creature Yields only when its block says so.
- Its tactics, in the book's words.
- A closing line with the VE its kill pays each participant of its Grade, and the Force it counts at in the sizing table from Level 8.

How to read a block is in Bestiary, "How to Read a Stat Block".

## Encounter sizing

The **Encounter sizing** panel on the right is the book's table (Bestiary, "GM Reference: Encounter Building"), with rows by Party Level and columns **Easy**, **Standard**, and **Hard**.

The line above the table describes the party the app reads: the living characters in the campaign and their average Level, rounded down (*4 at Level 8*). With no characters yet, it reads for four. The row for that Level is highlighted.

The table is written for a party of four. When yours is larger or smaller:

- From Level 8, each cell in the highlighted row shows the Force for your party size (*For 5: Force* and the number), which moves 10 Force per character.
- Below Level 8, a line under the table tells you to read one column easier or harder per character, or to drop or add one creature of the fight's lowest tier per character.

## Sizing a specific fight

The same table reads the creatures you choose when you set up a fight. In Combat's setup, the **Start** panel names the column your creatures land in for the characters you placed, lists this party's columns, and adds a note where the table stops short or leaves something out: a creature entered by hand with no tier or Forces, a creature outside the party's Grade (the Cross-Grade Adjustment applies), or a creature with other than two Beats. You can override the party Level there. See [Combat](guide:gm-combat).
