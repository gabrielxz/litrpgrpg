# HVE

The HVE section runs the Hidden Vector Engine: the session-end sweep, where you tally each character's moments and the record moves Current into Deep, and each character's standing sheet. Players never see anything here. The procedure is in The Hidden Vector Engine, "The Session-End Sweep".

![The HVE section: the sweep on the left, the standing sheets on the right.](gm-hve.webp)

## The session-end sweep

The panel headed **The session-end sweep** holds one sweep sheet per living character. Start by saying the session's three biggest moments out loud with the table; the tallies after that are yours alone.

Open **Weighing a moment** for the weights, what qualifies for each, an example, and the calibration rules. The weights are 1 (Remembered), 2 (Surprised the table), and 3 (Defining, circled).

### What fills a sheet

- **Logged since the last sweep** lists every HVE entry from [Events](guide:gm-events) not yet swept, with its event. Each is checked in by default. An entry logged at 0.5 is a reminder and starts unchecked; checking it raises it to a full tally of 1. Uncheck anything you do not want counted.
- Below that, add moments you recall. Pick the **Side** and **Weight**, **Also reads on** for a moment that reads on a second axis (weight 2 and above; the second side tallies one weight lower), and a **Note**. A weight 3 moment is circled and needs its **Margin note (required)**. Check **Coercion of a player character** for coercion aimed at another player's character, which is at least 2 tallies of Will. Press **Add**. **remove** takes a hand-added moment back off.
- Under each sheet, a table shows every axis with Current (as tallied so far) beside Deep (as it stands).

If the party's total passes 6 tallies, a warning notes that a session normally runs 3 to 6 and that more usually means table talk is being counted.

### Recording the sweep

1. Name the **Session**. While a session is running, its name is the placeholder; with none running, name the session the sweep closes.
2. Check the preview.
3. Press **Record the sweep**.

The record adds Current into Deep by the book's rule, clears Current, and marks the entries it took in as swept in Events. An entry you left unchecked stays for the next sweep. Moments you add by hand are kept in this browser until you record, so a reload does not lose them. If no moments come to mind, there is nothing to record and the sheets stay as they are.

Ending a session from [the table bar](guide:gm-session) warns you when no sweep was recorded for it and links here. Undo a sweep from the [Campaign log](guide:gm-log).

## Personal Opportunities

This panel drafts a Personal Opportunity offer through the campaign's AI key, after the sweep.

1. Choose who it is **For**.
2. Describe **The situation (optional)**: where they are, who is near, what presses on them.
3. Press **Draft an offer**.

The drafter works from the character's sheet, the sweep's moments, and the situation. When it finishes, the draft waits in [Suggestions](guide:gm-suggestions), where you open it in the Quests form to edit and issue. A character who has refused every flavor 6 times gets nothing more. Writing a Personal Opportunity by hand in [Quests](guide:gm-quests) makes the same record.

## Standing sheets

The right column shows every character's standing sheet:

- the Coherence profile and bonus (Leaning, Coherence +5) and the archetype the sheet reads as, when it reads as one
- Deep for each axis
- **Circled**: the margin notes of every weight 3 moment, with the session each came from
- **Sweeps**: every past sweep, newest first, with its moments and what it added to Deep

For a campaign that began on paper, open **Copy Deep across from a paper sheet**, type each side's tallies, and press **Set Kara's Deep**. The preview shows the change first.

What the engine drives (class generation, Principle crystallization, titles, the Breakthrough bonus) is in The Hidden Vector Engine, "What the Engine Drives".
