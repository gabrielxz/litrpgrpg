# Getting started

The companion app runs a Gradebreaker campaign at the table: the GM keeps the record, and each player sees their own character as the System shows it. This page covers signing in, the Home screen, joining a campaign, and building a character before you have one.

## Signing in

Open the app and press **Sign in with Google**. The name your Google account gives becomes your name at the table; you can change it on Home.

A development server also offers **Development sign-in**: type the name of a test person and press **Sign in**. Each browser tab signed in this way is its own person, and the top bar marks it with a **dev** tag.

**Sign out** sits at the right end of the top bar on every screen. **Guide**, beside your name, opens this guide in a tab of its own, at the page for the screen you are on.

## Home

![Home with the campaigns you run, the campaigns you play in, and your characters.](start-home.webp)

Home lists every campaign you belong to, in two panels.

- **Campaigns you run** lists the campaigns where you are the GM. Each shows the rules version it runs on.
- **Campaigns you play in** lists the campaigns you joined as a player. With none, it reads *None yet. Open an invite link from your GM to join one.*

Press a campaign's name to open it. A GM sees the GM's screen and a player sees the player's screen; the same person can be the GM of one campaign and a player in another.

### Starting a campaign

Type a name in **New campaign name** and press **Start a campaign**. You become its GM, and the campaign opens on the rules version shown under the field. [Setting up a campaign](guide:gm-setup) takes it from there.

### Your name at the table

**Your name at the table** is the name the GM and the other players see in every campaign: in the Table section, in Heard, and in the listening bar. Edit it and press **Save**.

## Joining with an invite link

Your GM sends you a link that ends in `/join/` and a code. Open it in your browser.

![An invite link showing the campaign's name and the Join the campaign button.](start-join.webp)

- The page shows the campaign's name.
- If you are signed out, it reads *Sign in to join as a player.* with the sign-in buttons under it. After you sign in, you come back to the same page.
- Signed in, it reads *You are joining as* and your name. Press **Join the campaign**, and the campaign opens.

A link your GM revoked, one past its limit of people, or one past its expiry reads *This invite can no longer be used. Ask your GM for a new link.* A code that never existed reads *This invite link does not exist.* Opening a link for a campaign you already belong to takes you into it and uses up nothing.

Joining makes you a player. Your screen shows *You have no character in this campaign yet* until you build one there, bring one you built earlier, or the GM hands you one (see [Building a character](guide:player-character)).

## Building a character before a campaign

The **Your characters** panel on Home holds characters you built that are not in any campaign yet.

1. Press **Build a character**. The character builder opens in the panel (the steps are on [Building a character](guide:player-character)).
2. Press **Register** to keep it. It appears in the list with its Attributes, or *ready-made* for one of the book's ready-made characters.
3. When you belong to a campaign, pick it in the list beside the character and press **Bring into campaign**. The campaign opens with the character in it.

> Bringing a character into a campaign moves it there for good: it leaves **Your characters** and cannot come back out. Brought into a campaign you play in, it is yours to play; brought into a campaign you run, the GM holds it.

To remove a character from the list, press **Delete…**, then **Delete** and the character's name to confirm, or **Keep** and the name to back out. Deleting is permanent.
