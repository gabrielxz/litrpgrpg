# Asking the rules

The Rules section answers a question about the rules from the book, and names the chapter and heading each part of the answer comes from. Open it from **Rules** in the Table group of the section tabs.

![The Rules section with a question asked and its answer citing the book.](gm-rules.webp)

## Asking

Type the question in the **Ask the rules** field, up to 1000 characters, and press **Ask** or Enter (Shift and Enter start a new line). The button reads **Looking it up…** while the answer comes.

Each answer appears as a panel under the question, newest first:

- the question, as you asked it;
- the answer, in plain prose: the rule, then the numbers it needs;
- the chapters and headings it draws on, as The Hidden Vector Engine, "The Session-End Sweep". The app keeps only citations that match a real chapter title and heading in the book.

When the book does not answer the question, the panel carries the tag *Not answered in the book: the GM's call.* The answer then says which chapter comes closest, if one does. A question about your table's story gets the rule, and the rest is left to you.

## What it reads

Your questions read every chapter of the book. A player's questions, asked from the **At the table** tray on their screen, read only the chapters players read: Introduction, Core Mechanics, Character Creation, Progression, Classes, The Principle System, Cultivation, Grade Breakthroughs, The Hidden Vector Engine, What Can Be Seen, and Quick Reference. Neither carries anything from the campaign, so an answer cannot tell a player what their screen does not already show (see [Asking the rules, for players](guide:player-rules)).

## What it keeps

Nothing. An answer stays on the screen that asked, up to the last 10, until the page reloads or you leave the section. No answer is recorded, and neither you nor a player sees the other's questions.

## What it needs

Asking runs on the campaign's AI key, and players' questions spend it as well. Without a key, the section says answering needs the campaign's key in place of the field. The AI card's **Usage** table counts the questions under **rules-question** (see [Setting up a campaign](guide:gm-setup)).

> The book is the rulebook, and the answer is a reading of it. Where the answer and the book differ, the book wins.
