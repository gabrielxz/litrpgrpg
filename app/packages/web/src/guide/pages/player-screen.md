# Your screen

Your screen is your character's System interface: what the System shows you about yourself, the notices it sends, and a tray of table tools. Most of it fills in from what the GM records. The choices the book gives to you, you make here.

![A player's screen: the interface at the left, the notices and the At the table tray at the right.](player-screen.webp)

The interface speaks as the System, in the world's words. The tray on the right speaks as the table: rounds, Beats, dice. The thin strip above everything is the listening strip; see [Listening](guide:player-listening).

## The head and vitals

The top of the interface stays in view while the rest scrolls. It shows your name, Level, Grade, and class once you hold one, then four bars:

- progress to the next Level (it reads "Grade limit" when you cannot level further at this Grade);
- **Health**;
- **Aether**;
- **Volatile Energy**, stored against your tolerance.

When your character is Downed, a line above the bars reads *Vital coherence: 3. Falling.* with three segments that go out one by one. See [Moments](guide:player-moments).

## Attributes

Each Attribute shows its raw score and, below it, its Force (the number you roll with). When free points wait, a line reads *Unallocated points* with a count, and each Attribute gets a **−** and **+** stepper. Place the points and press **Allocate**. You can leave points unplaced and come back to them. See [Moments](guide:player-moments) for level-ups.

## The rest of the interface

Each part appears once it has something in it.

- **Proficiencies**: each weapon shape you have a Mark in, with its tier and bonus.
- **Classification** and **Class**: your class offers, then the class you hold. See [Moments](guide:player-moments).
- **Principle**: resonance, Battle Memories, and your Principle once it has a name.
- **Titles**: every title you hold, hidden ones included. **Hidden: wear it** and **Worn: hide it** switch whether others can see a Bestowed title. **Reveal…** shows a Hidden Achievement to observers for good; it asks you to confirm first, because it cannot be taken back. A title that lets you choose where its point goes shows a list of Attributes and a **Place** button with the amount.
- **Quest log**: each quest in the System's format. Answer an offered quest with **Accept** or **Refuse**. **Share with the party** appears on a quest you can share. A Mandate can be refused with **Refuse…**, which warns you that it has consequences before you confirm. Closed quests fold under a line at the bottom.
- **Carried**: your items, and how many healing and Aether pills you have taken since your last Consolidation. Pick where an item goes and press **Hand over one** to give it to another character or to the spoils. A pill shows **Take one**; anything else shows **Used one**.
- **Party**: your party's Health and Aether. Pick a character and press **Invite Joe**. An invitation to you shows **Accept** and **Decline**; one you sent shows **Withdraw**. **Leave…** asks you to confirm before you go.
- **Inspect**: look at another being. See [Moments](guide:player-moments).

No other player sees your interface, and the GM sees all of it. Other players see your name, your rolls, and what inspection reads of your titles; members of your party also see your Health and Aether. The rules for who sees what are in What Can Be Seen.

Anything you press here records at once. If you press something by mistake, tell your GM, who can undo it.

## Notices

The **Notices** panel lists what the System tells you, newest first. The newest 5 read brightest; after 8, the rest fold under **Earlier notices**. A long notice shows its first paragraph, with **Read the rest** for the others. If you play more than one character in the campaign, each notice names the character it is about.

## At the table

The tray under the notices holds the table's tools.

- The fight, under its name, while one is running. See [Fights](guide:player-fight).
- **Spoils**, when the party has loot it has not divided. Pick who it is for and press **Take one**.
- **Seen**, once the GM has shown you an image or a handout, newest first. An image the GM shows while your screen is open fills the screen; press **Close**, Escape, or anywhere around it to put it away. Press any image in the strip to see it full-screen again.
- **Dice**, for any roll the GM calls for. See [Fights](guide:player-fight).
- **Ask the rules**. See [Asking the rules](guide:player-rules).

While a fight runs, **Dice** and **Ask the rules** fold closed so the fight stays in reach. Open them with a press on their heading.
