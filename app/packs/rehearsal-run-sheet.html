Past the Depot Fence

One night, two hours: the first for the table and its microphones, the second for one hour of play that ends on class offers. What to say, what the players do, and which button to press when. Tick each step as you go; the ticks stay in this browser.

  • SayRead aloud or paraphrase.
  • SystemWhat the players' screens show.
  • PlayersWhat they do on their own screens.
  • IfA branch; skip it if it does not happen.
  • Button Press this, exactly as labeled.
  • Section Where the button is.

Before the nightGet ready

A day or two ahead

  1. A solo dry run, 20 minutes. On Home, start a campaign called Dry run. In Prep, under Load a pack from a file, choose app/packs/rehearsal.yaml, press Load rehearsal, then Record the setup. The four characters stay Held by the GM, so you act for all of them.

    Run Scene 2's fight from this sheet playing every side, settle it, then do the rest and one set of class offers. That is every hard button of the night, once, with nobody waiting.

  2. Send the players: the guide's Your screen and Listening pages (gradebreaker.fly.dev/guide/player-screen and /guide/player-listening), and a note to join on a laptop in Chrome, with headphones.

  3. Open a notes file beside the app. One line whenever a draft is late, invents something, or misses something plain; whenever the app wanted a click the table would not wait for; whenever a player could not find something; and the clock time at each scene's start.

The first hourThe table, microphones, and trouble

Up to an hour; whatever is left over goes to play

  1. Before anyone joins: on Home, start a campaign called Past the depot fence. Table On the AI card, paste your Anthropic key, Check and save, wait for Works. Prep Load a pack from a file: rehearsal.yaml, Load rehearsal, Record the setup. Kara, Joe, Andre, and Nia now stand at Level 9, and the clock reads Day 12, 16:00.

  2. Table Under Invite links, type 4, press New invite link, then Copy, and paste it into the table's chat.

    PlayersOpen the link, sign in with Google, press Join the campaign. Their screen says they have no character yet; that is expected.

  3. Table Under Who plays whom, pick each character's player in Played by and press the Hand Kara to … button that appears. All four.

    Say"You should each see your character now. The big panel is your System interface. The tray on the right, At the table, is where the fight, the dice, and the loot will be."

  4. Say"The app can listen to us and draft what happened for me to check; testing that is half of tonight. There's a strip at the top of your screen. Press Consent to listening, read it, and say yes if you're happy with it. If you're willing, Test recordings keeps your audio for seven days so I can measure how well it hears us."

    Press Consent to listening… in your own table bar too, then I consent.

  5. Party The Titles due card lists Week One for all four. Press Confer on each row.

    SystemTitle conferred: Week One.

    PlayersUnder Titles, pick an Attribute for the +1 and press Place. Their first press of the night; watch who finds it.

  6. Table bar: Start a session, name it The depot gate, leave all four ticked, Start The depot gate. Then Start listening. If it reads Waiting on consent from …, that player has not consented yet. Once it shows Listening, press Record for testing.

  7. Mic check. Open Events; the Heard this session card is at the top.

    Say"One line each, in character: your name, and what you did before the System came."

    Each line should appear under the right name within a few seconds. If a player's lines do not appear, open the table bar's listening drawer: it names each person's state (muted, no microphone, nothing arriving).

    Iflistening will not work for someone after ten minutes, play without it for them, or for everyone. The drafting then covers fewer lines; nothing else changes.

The hour · Scene 1 · 5 minutesThe gate

Note the clock time. From here on, ignore the Suggestions and Events counts until the rest.

  1. Prep On Day 12: the status notice, press Send…, leave everyone ticked, Send the notice.

    SystemDay 12 since Integration. Sector: Depot District. Initiates in range: 4. Local hostiles: present.

  2. SayThe depot district smells of diesel and wet concrete. A chain-link fence runs the length of the freight yard, sagging where something heavy leaned on it. At the gate stands a woman in her sixties with a crowbar and a co-op armband. She watches the fence line more than she watches you.

  3. Play Mara. Ask one character, by name, and note who.

    Say, as Mara"My grandson went past that fence yesterday with two others. Teo. Nineteen. They were after the first-aid locker in the depot office. They didn't come back. Bring them home."

    Issue nothing for this. It is the listener's test: at the start of Scene 3 you will look for it in Suggestions.

  4. SayThree shapes move along the fence line, low to the ground, heads down. Dog-sized, but the jaws are wrong: too wide, with a second row of teeth behind the first. One stops and looks straight at you.

    Prep On [Q-201] Fence Line Clearance, press Issue…. The Quests form opens filled in. Offer it to whoever is nearest the fence, Issue [Q-201].

    PlayersThat player presses Accept in their quest log, then Share with the party. Say it out loud.

Scene 2 · 25 minutesThe fence line

Three Snarljaws: 24 HP each, two attacks a turn, +10 when another shares the Zone

  1. Prep On The fence line: three Snarljaws, press Set up the fight. Combat opens filled in. Check each character's side reads The party, then Start the fight and Roll Initial Momentum.

    Say"The fight is in your tray now. When your side has the turn, press Act, then Attack…, pick a target and your Attribute, and attack. When something attacks you, your tray asks how you defend: press Roll the Clash, and if you lose, choose to take it or Yield Beats. I'll tell you what the numbers mean."

  2. A Snarljaw's turn, on the Hostiles' turn, for each:

    • On its row, Snarljaw 1 acts, then Attack….
    • Pick the target; the bite fills in, and Flanking +10 ticks itself. Snarljaw 1 attacks (1 Beat).
    • The Clash docks on the right. The player defends and chooses on their screen; if they are stuck, press Roll the Clash for them.
    • Second Beat: attack again, or after a hit, drag the prey down (open the target's row, Exposed). Then Done.

    When every side has acted, Next round.

  3. Ifthe fight passes 20 minutes, the last Snarljaw breaks and runs: press Out on its row. The VE plan holds with two fled.

  4. End the fight…, then End The fence line: three Snarljaws.

    If it refusesa character is Downed and dying. An ally uses Stabilize…, or on the Downed row press Stabilized under Your ruling:.

  5. The aftermath shows each kill, its finishing blow, and 10 VE each. Settle The fence line: three Snarljaws.

    SystemKill confirmed. Grade F, Moderate. Volatile Energy absorbed: 10.

    Ifone character killed all three, Pack-Breaker appears under Titles due on Party: Confer.

  6. Quests On [Q-201], press +1 once per kill, then Complete…, Complete [Q-201] (8 VE each).

  4. SayMara sees Teo before he reaches the gate. She drops the crowbar.

    Quests On [Q-202] Bring Them Home, Complete…. Every holder shows 12 VE. Complete [Q-202].

    Checkall four hold it. One who does not gets nothing from it.

The rest · 20 minutesConsolidation, and the classes

  1. Say"Mara takes you in for the night. Six hours of rest. Give me five minutes at the table while I catch up; then the System has something for you."

  2. Review the drafts: five minutes.

    • Events Under To review, Accept what happened, Dismiss what did not. Write a line for each wrong one.
    • Suggestions Accept or dismiss what is there (a title, a Battle Memory Card, a Hidden Achievement).

  3. Say"Quick one: what were the three biggest moments tonight?"

    HVE The entries you accepted are already on each sheet. Add a moment the table named that is missing, then Record the sweep. The class offers read what this adds.

  4. Party Record panel, Consolidation: all four ticked, Process everything, Hours 6 each, keep the clock box ticked, Record the rest and move the clock 6 h.

    SystemLevel 10 attained. Level 10. Classification available.

  5. Classes Each character has a panel … is due three class offers. Start all four drafts first, then come back to each:

    • Open Draft the three offers from the record, type one line under What the player keeps doing that no rule asked for, press Draft three offers. Move to the next character while it runs.
    • When a draft lands: Load them into the three offers, read the three, fix any line that sounds wrong, and Offer Kara these three classes.

    Say(while the drafts run) "While the System decides what you are: what did your character want tonight that they didn't get?" Their answers make good one-liners for the box above.

    Ifa draft is poor or slow: in each of the three editors, Start from a book class instead. The list marks the ones that fit the character.

    PlayersRead the three cards under Classification, press Accept… on one, then confirm. Give them the time; this is the night's payoff.

The close · 5 minutesWrapping up

  1. Say"Thank you. Two questions: was there anything on your screen you couldn't find? And did anything feel slow?" Write the answers down.

  2. Table bar: End the session, Draft it from the record, read it, End The depot gate. Listening and the test recording stop with it.

  3. After the players go: Table Test recordings, download each person's track and the Timeline. The server deletes them after 7 days.

When it goes sidewaysTrouble

You pressed the wrong thing
Campaign log Find the entry, Undo, read the preview, confirm. In a fight, the tracker's own Undo takes back the last tracker action.
Nothing happens when anyone presses in the fight
A Clash is waiting in the right column: someone must defend, or choose Take or Yield. Press Roll the Clash or pick their choice yourself.
The fight will not end
Someone is Downed and dying. Your ruling: Stabilized on their row settles it.
A player cannot find something
Player view Pick their name to see exactly their screen, and tell them where it is.
Listening stopped
The table bar says why: someone withdrew, or someone arrived without consent. Fix it, then Start listening.
The AI is slow or down
Issue Bring Them Home from Prep, skip the draft review, and write the class offers from Start from a book class.
You are running out of time
Cut in this order: the loot, the sweep, the draft review. Keep the rest and the class offers.
