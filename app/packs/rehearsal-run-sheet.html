Past the Depot Fence

The rehearsal, step by step: what to say, what the players do, and which button to press when. Tick each step as you go; the ticks stay in this browser.

Is it easy enough?

Not as one sitting. The rehearsal was written to touch every system once: four fights, two rests, a level, class offers, a Distillation, a Mandate, and listening throughout. Run straight through for the first time, that is a five-hour session with a lot of new buttons. It becomes comfortable with four changes, and this sheet is built around them:

  1. Two sittings. Part 1 is the gate, the fence, and the first rest (about two and a half hours). Part 2 is the annex, the three upstairs, the classes, and the terminal. If Part 1 runs well, carry on the same night.
  2. Review drafts only at the rests. While a scene runs, ignore the Suggestions and Events counts. The rests and the close have a step for it.
  3. A solo dry run first. Twenty minutes alone with the fence fight, below, so the first fight at the table is your second.
  4. Cut what the plan does not need. The Alpha in the lot runs only if the party talks the Sentinel down (it replaces that VE). Andre's Distillation happens only if his Insight gets there. Scene 5 can move to another night.
  • SayRead aloud or paraphrase.
  • SystemWhat the players' screens show.
  • PlayersWhat they do on their own screens.
  • IfA branch; skip it if it does not happen.
  • Button Press this, exactly as labeled.
  • Section Where the button is.

Before the dayGet ready

A day or two ahead

  1. Solo dry run. On Home, start a campaign called Dry run. In Prep, under Load a pack from a file, choose app/packs/rehearsal.yaml, press Load rehearsal, then Record the setup. The four characters stay Held by the GM, so you can act for all of them.

    Then run Scene 1's fight from this sheet, playing every side: Prep Set up the fight on the fence line, set each character's side to The party, Start the fight, Roll Initial Momentum, and take a few turns with Kara acts and Attack…. End it, settle it, and record one rest. That is every hard button in Part 1.

  2. Send the players two things: the guide's pages Your screen and Listening (gradebreaker.fly.dev/guide/player-screen and /guide/player-listening), and a note to join on a laptop in Chrome, with headphones so the table's voices do not reach each other's microphones.

  3. Open a notes file beside the app for the rehearsal's real purpose: a line whenever a draft arrives late, invents something, or misses something plain; whenever the app wanted a click the table did not want to wait for; and whenever a player could not find something.

Scene 1The depot gate

About 45 minutes · Prep group "Scene 1: The depot gate"

  1. Prep On Day 12: the status notice, press Send…, leave everyone ticked, and press Send the notice.

    SystemDay 12 since Integration. Sector: Depot District. Initiates in range: 4. Local hostiles: present.

  2. SayThe depot district smells of diesel and wet concrete. A chain-link fence runs the length of the freight yard, sagging where something heavy leaned on it. At the gate stands a woman in her sixties with a crowbar and a co-op armband. She watches the fence line more than she watches you.

  3. Play Mara. Pick one character to ask, by name.

    Say, as Mara"My grandson went past that fence yesterday with two others. Teo. Nineteen. They said the records annex still had power. They didn't come back. You look like you can handle yourself. Bring them home."

    Ifthey agree: leave it to the listener. At the first rest you will look for a drafted Bring Them Home quest in Suggestions. If listening is off, issue it now from Prep: [Q-202] Bring Them Home Issue…, offer it to the character Mara asked, and press Issue [Q-202].

  4. SayThree shapes move along the fence line, low to the ground, heads down. Dog-sized, but the jaws are wrong: too wide, with a second row of teeth behind the first. One stops and looks straight at you.

    Prep On [Q-201] Fence Line Clearance, press Issue…. The Quests form opens filled in. Offer it to whoever is nearest the fence and press Issue [Q-201].

    PlayersThat player presses Accept in their quest log, then Share with the party. Say so out loud; it is the first thing a player does on their screen tonight.

  5. Prep On The fence line: three Snarljaws, press Set up the fight. Combat opens filled in with the three Snarljaws and the Zones (the gate, the fence line, the yard). Check each character's side reads The party, then press Start the fight.

    Press Roll Initial Momentum. The side that wins acts first.

    Say"Your screens have the fight now, in the At the table tray. When your side has the turn, press Act, then Attack…, pick a target and your Attribute, and press attack. When something attacks you, your screen asks how you defend: press Roll the Clash, and if you lose, choose to take it or Yield Beats. I'll narrate what the numbers mean."

  6. Running a Snarljaw's turn (repeat for each, on the Hostiles' turn):

    • On its row, press Snarljaw 1 acts, then Attack….
    • Pick the target; its bite fills in. Flanking +10 ticks itself when another Snarljaw shares the Zone. Press Snarljaw 1 attacks (1 Beat).
    • The Clash docks on the right. Wait while the player defends and chooses; or press Roll the Clash yourself if they are stuck.
    • It has 2 Beats: attack again, or after a hit, drag the prey down instead (open the target's row and press Exposed). Then Done.

    When every side has acted, press Next round.

  7. Set up Scene 2's test. When a Snarljaw is badly hurt, describe it backing away, belly low, whining.

    SayThe last one stops fighting. It backs toward the gap in the fence, one foreleg held up, eyes on you.

    Ifa character lets it go: press Out on its row (it fled; no kill). Remember who spared it. That character gets the Hidden quest in Scene 2.

  8. Press End the fight…, then End The fence line: three Snarljaws.

    If it refusesa character is Downed and dying. Someone must stabilize them (their Stabilize…), or run the rounds out. See Trouble, below.

  9. The aftermath panel shows each kill, who landed the finishing blow, and 10 VE each. Check it reads right and press Settle The fence line: three Snarljaws.

    SystemKill confirmed. Grade F, Moderate. Volatile Energy absorbed: 10.

    Ifone character killed all three, Pack-Breaker appears under Titles due on Party. Press Confer.

  10. Quests On [Q-201], press +1 once per Snarljaw killed. Then Complete… and Complete [Q-201] (8 VE each; it completes even if one fled).

When it goes sidewaysTrouble

You pressed the wrong thing
Campaign log Find the entry, press Undo, read the preview, confirm. In a fight, the tracker's own Undo takes back the last tracker action.
The fight will not end
A character is Downed and dying. An ally uses Stabilize… or a heal, or you press Next round until it resolves. On the Downed row, Your ruling: Stabilized settles it at once if the fiction supports it.
Nothing happens when a player presses
A Clash is waiting. Look at the right column: someone must defend or choose Take or Yield first. Press Roll the Clash or pick their choice yourself.
A player cannot find something
Player view Pick their name and you see exactly their screen. Tell them where it is; write it in your notes.
Listening stopped
The table bar says why: the session ended, someone withdrew, or a character arrived whose player has not consented. Fix that, then Start listening.
A draft is wrong
Dismiss it and note it. Wrong drafts are the rehearsal's most useful output.
The AI is slow or down
Everything has a manual path: log an event by hand in Events, write class offers from Start from a book class, write the summary yourself.
You are behind on time
Skip the composer, Andre's Distillation, the Personal Opportunity, and Scene 5. The level and the classes are the parts worth keeping.
