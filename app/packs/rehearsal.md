# The rehearsal: past the depot fence

One night, two hours at most: the first hour for joining, microphones, and anything technical, the second for one hour of play that ends on class offers. The aim is a general sense of how the app holds up at a real table, not a pass over every feature. It spoils nothing in the tutorial. The pack is `rehearsal.yaml` beside this sheet; the GM's step-by-step script, with what to say and which button to press, is `rehearsal-run-sheet.html`.

The party is four Initiates at Level 9 on Day 12, eleven days after Integration, in a depot district outside any tutorial valley: Kara, Joe, Andre (the book's pregens, leveled), and Nia, an ER nurse. Each has 100 of the 120 VE toward Level 10 refined and nothing stored, and each carries eleven days of HVE tallies, so the class offers have something to read.

## The first hour: the table

1. Create the campaign, enter the model key on the Table section's AI card, and load `rehearsal.yaml` in Prep (Load a pack from a file, then Record the setup). Speech-to-text runs on the server's own key.
2. Invite the four players. In the Table section's Who plays whom, give each a character.
3. Each player consents to listening, and to test recordings if they agree.
4. Confer Week One on all four from the Titles due card; each player places the point on their own screen. It is their first press, and it shows them the interface.
5. Start the session, start listening, start "Record for testing", and check each microphone in the Heard card.

Left out tonight, each still built and worth its own session: the Hidden quest and its reveal, Battle Memory meditation and Principle naming, Distillation, a Mandate on the clock, a second fight with an NPC ally or a creature that Yields, cores and Attribute Treasures, Saturation, the System message composer, Personal Opportunities, and the campaign memory.

## The VE plan

The pack's test plays these numbers through the record, so a rules change that breaks them fails the check. Three Moderate kills pay 10 each to every participant (30), Fence Line Clearance pays 8, and Bring Them Home (Faction, Easy) pays 12: 50 stored each, under the Tolerance of 80. The 6-hour rest refines them, and everyone reaches Level 10. It still holds with two Snarljaws fled: one kill and Bring Them Home alone pay the 20 each needs.

## What to watch

The GM writes a line for each of these, for the notes after:

- Drafts that arrived late, or missed something the table plainly did.
- Drafts that invented something, especially from jokes and rules questions.
- Every place the app needed a click the table did not want to wait for.
- Anything a player could not find on their own screen.
- How long the fight and the class offers took, against the minutes above.
