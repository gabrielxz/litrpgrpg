The Tutorial, One Player

Phases 1 and 2 with one player and you: the void, the landing, one encounter, the arrival fight, and the walk down toward the Node. About an hour, with the app checked along the way. Written for the first player with The Locked Cache; the other seven encounters' presses are at the end, for the next players. The book (The Tutorial) wins wherever this sheet and it differ.

  • SayRead aloud or paraphrase.
  • SystemWhat the player's screen shows.
  • PlayerWhat they do on their own screen.
  • IfA branch; skip it if it does not happen.
  • Button Press this, exactly as labeled.
  • Section Where the button is.
  • ImageA picture to show: its card in Prep, then Show… and Show the image, with their character ticked.

Before the nightGet ready

  1. Pick the player: the one most patient with a stall, since tonight is also the app's first real night. Tell them it runs about an hour, one-on-one, on a laptop in Chrome with headphones, and that they will build their character at the start.

  2. Choose their probe and encounter. This sheet scripts probe 1 (three sounds, the third their name said wrong) with The Locked Cache: it uses the most of the app (a System notice from Prep, a check, loot, a VE award) and its arrival is a Husk Crawler in the wreck, which probe 1's sounds set up. For another player, pick a different pair; their presses are under Other encounters.

  3. Open a notes file beside the app: a line whenever the app stalls you, whenever the player cannot find something, and whenever a draft is late, wrong, or missing. Note the clock time at each phase.

Setup · before the player joinsThe campaign

  1. On Home, start the campaign under its real name. Table On the AI card, paste your Anthropic key, Check and save, wait for Works.

  2. Prep On the tutorial pack panel, press Load the tutorial pack. Its notices, fights, loot, NPCs, and images appear grouped by phase. Phase 1 and Phase 2 are the groups tonight. An image card shows the picture; its cue says when to show it.

  3. Table Under Invite links, type 1, New invite link, Copy, and send it to the player. Later players get their own links.

With the player · 10 to 15 minutesJoining and the character

  1. PlayerOpens the link, signs in with Google, presses Join the campaign. Their screen reads Interface: awaiting registration.

  2. Say"Press Build a character and choose Build your own. Your character is you, or someone like you, on an ordinary day: a name, one or two lines about your life and work, and 40 points across seven Attributes, each from 3 to 10. The line under each number tells you what it means. Don't overthink it."

    PlayerFills the form and presses Register. Their interface appears at Level 1.

    Ifthey would rather not build one tonight: Play a ready-made character and pick Kara, Joe, or Andre.

  3. Say"One more thing: what were you wearing, and what's in your pockets right now, at this moment on an ordinary day?"

    Ifthey name something useful (a pocketknife, a lighter, a phone): Party Record panel Items, Give, to their character, type it, and record it. The System provides nothing else; everything after this is scavenged.

  4. Say"The app can listen to us and draft what happened for me to check; I'm testing that tonight. There's a strip at the top of your screen: Consent to listening. Read it and say yes if you're happy with it. Test recordings is separate: it keeps your audio seven days so I can measure how well it hears."

    Press Consent to listening… in your own table bar too, then I consent.

  5. Table bar: Start a session, name it Arrival: [the character's name], keep them ticked, press Start Arrival: …. Then Start listening, and once it shows Listening, Record for testing.

    Say"Say a sentence about your character, as yourself." Watch Events: the line should appear in Heard this session under their name. If it does not, the table bar's listening drawer names the problem.

  6. Say"It's an ordinary day. You're doing whatever you'd be doing at this hour. Tell me where you are." Let them answer in a sentence, then begin. Say nothing about the world: the void is the introduction.

Phase 1 · 2 to 5 minutesThe Threshold

Keep it short. The character should stay disoriented.

  1. Read aloud: the voidBetween one moment and the next, the world goes.

    No flash, and no sound of anything breaking. The ground is gone from under you, and then the direction the ground used to be is gone too. You reach out. There is no arm to reach with, and something reaches anyway.

    You cannot feel your body. You can still think, and when you try to move, something answers.

    Far off, in a direction you have no name for, there are others. Thousands of them, more, felt the way a crowd is felt in a dark stadium.

    Then something notices you. All of it at once, the way the ocean would notice a swimmer.

    ImageAs you begin reading: Prep The void Show…, Show the image. It fills their screen.

    Say, the first time"Pictures open over your screen. Close one when you're ready, with Escape or a click around it; it stays under Seen in your tray."

    Prep On The Void (Phase 1), press Send…, keep their character ticked, Send the notice. Read it aloud as it lands.

    SystemConsciousness anchored. Native world: Earth. Status: Integrated. Population: 7,916,442,203. Integration stability: provisional. Tutorial: provisioned. Prior exemptions: expired. Observation: begun.

    If they ask the System a question, nothing answers.

  2. Read aloud: the probeSomewhere below you something is repeating. Three sounds, the same three, over and over, and the third one is your name said wrong.

    Then wait. There is no prescribed answer. Note what they do: go toward it, hold still and work out the pattern, or something else.

  3. The System's private line. Party Record panel System message: tick their character, and under The System says type the line that fits what they did:

    • went toward it: Approach logged. Threshold tolerance: above baseline.
    • pulled back: Withdrawal logged. Risk profile: conservative. Revising estimate.
    • kept listening, working it out: Repeat engagement logged. Fixation: probable.
    • did nothing: No response recorded. Recording anyway.

    Send it with the panel's send button. It is the first System message you write yourself.

  4. Read aloud: the fallThe dark lets go all at once. There is ground, and it hits you before you know you are falling: grit, heat, the taste of scorched metal. Above you, a sky the wrong color.

    Your ears come back last. Far off across the rubble, other things are hitting the ground, a scatter of impacts too spread out to count.

    ImagePrep The landing Show…, Show the image.

    Prep The Fall Send…, Send the notice.

    SystemIntegration complete. Vitals: nominal.

    Ifthey went toward the sounds: they land facing the thing that made them, and see it first. If they held still and worked out the pattern: they land knowing its rhythm, and will know the moment it changes. Carry that into the arrival.

Phase 2 · 10 to 20 minutesThe Violent Arrival: The Locked Cache

  1. Read aloud: the valleyYou wake on a slope of broken masonry, none of it from the same building, all of it shifting a little as you stand.

    You are on the inside rim of a valley, and the valley is made of parts. Below you a forest is giving off its own light, blue, moving when there is no wind. Beyond it, at the bottom where everything comes to rest, a gray hill of debris stands with paths of wreckage fanning out from it like something poured.

    Around the rim, far off, other things are still hitting the ground in little bursts of dust. After a moment, one of them gets up.

    Nothing here grew together. Somebody put it here.

    ImagePrep The valley Show…, Show the image, as you finish reading.

    Ifthey look around: a line of rain to the north standing still as a wall, a tower to the west hanging in the pieces it broke into, windows to the south, and behind them, past the rim, a wall of moving light where the horizon should be. Every path of wreckage runs down to the hill.

  2. Read aloud: the cacheA box stands upright in the debris. Clean. Humming. There is no dust on it and everything around it is buried.

    Three things are set into its face: a narrow slot the width of a blade, a shallow dish, and a panel of symbols that is either counting up or counting down. Forty feet to your right, a heap of wreckage is still burning.

    When your hand comes near the dish, text arrives behind your eyes.

    ImagePrep The box in the debris Show…, Show the image, as you read.

    When their hand nears the dish: Prep The Locked Cache Send…, Send the notice.

    SystemThermal input required. Threshold: 40 degrees. Current reading: 36.6. Insufficient.

    Read aloudBehind you, deeper in the wreck, a sheet of metal clatters down flat, the way it would if something stepped on it.

  3. Any one of three inputs opens it (yours only):

    • Heat: a hot fragment from the burning wreck, pressed into the dish. Automatic if they think of the wreck; if they ask what nearby is hot, a PER check against 65.
    • Blade: any hard edge fits the slot.
    • Symbols: watching thirty seconds shows it counts seconds since Integration; pressing it as the count lands on a multiple of 100 opens it. Tell them when the next one is due.
    • Force it: STR check against 115. It opens; the buckler and the tablet are crushed, the pill survives.

    Say, for a check"Open Dice in your tray. Check, pick Perception (or Strength), and roll." Compare their total to the number above, and tell them what happens. That teaches the roll before the fight does.

    Ifthey work slowly, the creature arrives first: run the arrival, and they can open the cache afterward.

  4. When it opens: Prep The Locked Cache: its contents Give…, choose their character, Give it to …. Forced open: Record panel Items, Give, the Lesser Healing Pill alone.

    SystemItem registered: Reactive Buckler.

    ImageOpened clean: Prep A reactive buckler Show…, Show the image, and A sparkstone tablet the same way. Forced: Healing pills.

    Then the reward: Record panel Award VE, Source Other (note: the Locked Cache), their character, 30 for a clean open or 15 forced, Award 30 VE.

Phase 2 · the arrival · 10 minutesThe first fight

One Husk Crawler, Trivial, sized for one Level 1 character. Nobody dies in this fight.

  1. Read aloudThe thing that knocked the metal down comes out of the wreck low and crooked: a body of plates and wire the size of a big dog, dragging one leg, its head turning in short jerks. It stops. The head turns toward you, and stays.

    ImagePrep It comes out of the wreck Show…, Show the image, as you read. The thing in the wreck is the creature alone, for later.

    Prep On The arrival: rubble, slopes, ruins, press Set up the fight. Set their character's side to The party, Start the fight, Roll Initial Momentum.

    Ifprobe 1 put them facing it, or they spotted it first: before rolling Momentum, open An ambush? Give the Surprise Beat and tick their character. They act once before anything else.

  2. Step 1: roll and see what happens. Do not explain the Clash first.

    Say"It's coming. On your screen, in the tray, press Act, then Attack…. Pick it as the target, and for Attack with pick Strength if you're swinging something, Dexterity if you're throwing it. Then attack."

    When the Crawler attacks: on its row Husk Crawler acts, Attack…, target them, Husk Crawler attacks (1 Beat). Their tray asks how they defend.

    Say"It's swinging at you. In your tray, pick how you defend: Dexterity to dodge, Fortitude to take it on something solid. Then Roll the Clash." Narrate the result in the world; give the damage without the formula.

  3. Step 2: Beats and Zones, when they ask whether they can do two things, or at the start of their second turn.

    Say"You get two Beats a turn: an attack is one, moving to the next area is one. You can see them as pips by your name. The areas are Zones; it's in yours."

  4. Step 3: the Surge, the first time they want an attack badly.

    Say"You can push. Tick Surge before you roll: it spends some Aether for +5 on this roll. Aether only comes back after the first full hour of rest, and that's hours away; you have enough for at least two of these."

    Whatever they choose, say nothing about it.

  5. Steps 4 and 5: the creature wins one, and Yield. When a hit would drop them, their tray shows both totals, the Margin, and buttons: Take 31, Yield 1 Beat: 11, Yield 2 Beats: 0. Before they press:

    Say, with the numbers on their screen"That's a Margin of 31 and you have 12 Health. Before it lands: you can give ground. Give up one Beat from your next turn and the Margin drops by 20. Give up both and it drops by 40; your next turn is gone, and it can push you out of this Zone. What do you do?"

    Then wait. Do not recommend an answer. This is the first Yield decision at your table, and they make it alone.

    Ifthe dice give them a clean win, teach Yield after the fight with the same words and the book's example (Dana, Margin 31).

  6. If they go Downedthey will not die here. Describe the Crawler losing interest, or starting to drag them, and someone's path crossing theirs. On their row, under Your ruling:, press Stabilized, then Out on the Crawler. They wake at 1 Health when the fight ends.

  7. When it dies: End the fight…, End The arrival: rubble, slopes, ruins, then in the aftermath Settle ….

    ImagePrep What comes off it Show…, Show the image, as you begin the read-aloud. Going to Prep leaves the aftermath waiting in Combat.

    Read aloud: the first killIt stops moving all at once, the way a machine does.

    Then something comes off it. Pale, slow, like heat over a road, except that it does not rise. It leans toward you, crosses the ground, and goes in through your chest. Warmth spreads down through your arms and legs.

    SystemKill confirmed. Grade F, Trivial. Volatile Energy absorbed: 2.

    Say"Look at your interface: Volatile Energy, 2 out of 80. That's what was holding that body together, and it's yours now. When you rest properly, it turns into progress toward your next level."

Phase 2 · the walk down · 5 minutesScavenging

  1. Read aloud: two or three of theseA spear stands upright where it fell, pinning a sheet of tarpaulin to the ground.

    A crystal the size of a thumb is fused into the masonry, glowing faintly through its own coat of dust.

    A white case with a red cross on it has slid to a stop against a wall. The cross is almost the right shape.

    ImageFor each find you describe: Prep A spear Show…, Show the image, A shard in the masonry, or A battered medkit.

    For each they take: Record panel Items, Give, their character, the name as the catalog suggests it (Spear, an Edge Shard or Pulse Shard for the crystal, Battered Medkit), and record it.

    Hold the rest of the book's list (armor scraps, the relic, the Resonance Glass, the Battered Communicator) for the other players' walks and the Node pile.

  2. Read aloudThe slope runs down toward the gray hill. Somewhere below, between you and it, something moves that walks like a person.

    Prep After the arrival Send…. Change 7 minutes to the minutes they actually spent since landing, then Send the notice.

    SystemSurvival duration: 12 minutes. First engagement: resolved. Behavioral telemetry: registered.

    End the phase there, on the walk down. The Node and the others are Phase 3, with the group.

The close · 10 minutesWrapping up

  1. Say, out of character"That's the end of your arrival; you'll meet the others at the bottom of the valley. Two questions: was there anything on your screen you couldn't find, or didn't understand? And did anything feel slow?" Write it down. Ask them not to tell the others what happened.

  2. The drafts. Events Under To review, accept what happened (the probe choice, how they opened the cache, the Yield decision), dismiss what did not, and note each wrong one. Suggestions Look, and accept or dismiss.

  3. HVE On their sheet, the accepted entries are checked in. Add what is missing from your own memory: the probe, the cache, the Yield. Weigh what they chose in light of what they knew; a choice made without knowing a rule says nothing about temperament. Record the sweep.

  4. Table bar: End the session, Draft it from the record, read it, End Arrival: …. Listening and the test recording stop with it.

    Afterward, Table Test recordings: download their track and the Timeline (kept 7 days).

Each encounter's image is named first; show it as you read. When it goes sidewaysTrouble

You pressed the wrong thing
Campaign log Find it, Undo, confirm. In the fight, the tracker's Undo takes back the last tracker action.
An image did not open on their screen
It opens only while their screen is open; it is still under Seen in their tray. Wrong image: Campaign log Undo takes it back off their screen.
Nothing happens in the fight
A Clash is waiting in the right column for a defense or a Take or Yield choice. Press Roll the Clash or the choice yourself.
The fight will not end
They are Downed. Your ruling: Stabilized.
The player cannot find something
Player view shows exactly their screen. Point them to it, and write it down.
Listening fails
Play on without it; you log moments by hand in Events or at the sweep. Write down what the drawer said.
The player builds a character that will not register
The line above Register names what is missing: a name, a Background, or points not spent to exactly 40.
