## Prompt

Create an original Gradebreaker illustration using the supplied approved office image as a style reference only. Preserve its angular cut-paper/gouache construction, selective tactile texture, strong light-dark masses, expressive human figures, and deliberately simplified but recognizable surroundings. Match its artistic character rather than copying its scene.

Build people through angular silhouettes and large expressive clothing planes. Keep them human and individually posed. Do not make them photorealistic, softly cartooned, mechanically low-poly, or uniformly jagged. Avoid adding detail merely because an object is present.

Anchor the physical world in bone ivory, gray, and blue-black, with the selected restrained scene colors. Cyan System effects should use precise invented glyph strings and purposeful geometry, with weight, density, and placement suited to the specified intent. Keep ordinary light and System emission distinct. Do not render readable text, numbers, logos, or conventional HUD microtext inside the illustration.

Use large quiet areas and meaningful background features. Let texture support shapes; do not coat the image in a filter. Follow the scene and placement brief below.

**Scene.** A city square at dusk, three days after a gate opened in it. The old courthouse stands on one side, and a doorway between worlds occupies its stone arch: the opening is not empty, and what shows through it is a different place under a different hour's light. On the near side, ordinary people have set up a folding table at the mouth of the arch and are working it with a clipboard, checking what comes through. Through the arch, on the far side, a freight yard: stacked crates and one non-human person standing beside them holding a manifest, waiting to be dealt with. The emotional purpose is an ordinary afternoon's bureaucracy conducted at the edge of something enormous, and the enormous thing is not finished.

**The non-human person.** Three weight-bearing legs arranged around a low torso, two manipulating arms above, and broad movable fans along either side of the head where a human has ears. Clothed and working. Constructed with the same angular dignity as the human figures, individually posed, legible as a person doing a job. Not a costume, not an insect, not a monster.

**Placement.** Full-page chapter opener.

**Format.** Portrait 2:3, 1024 by 1536 or the largest portrait available. Keep faces and focal glyphs clear of the outer 5% at top and bottom; the page crops there.

**Palette.** Bone ivory, gray, and blue-black foundation. Low warm dusk light on the near side of the arch. The light coming through the arch is a different hour and a cooler cast, so the boundary reads as a boundary without any edge effect drawn on it. Restrained scene color otherwise.

**System.** One vast cyan diagram standing in the sky over the square, occupying the upper portion of the frame. Recognition and advancement held short of completion: separate fragments that have resolved into alignment in places, with whole sections still unaligned or absent. Concentrated illumination on the parts that have resolved; low contrast and partial inscription where they have not. It is far away and enormous, and nobody in the square is looking at it. Fine cyan structure only, no halo, no central diamond, no ring.

**Must show.** The stone arch with the far side visible through it. The folding table and the people working it. The stacked freight and the three-legged person with the manifest beyond. The unfinished diagram overhead.

**May omit.** The rest of the square's buildings, the river and its bridges, crowd detail, vehicles, any signage.

**Do not show.** Anyone climbing the courthouse's front steps. Weapons drawn, combat, or a creature. Readable text, numbers, or logos of any kind.

**Reference roles.** The office image is the style anchor only. Do not copy its characters, its composition, or its glyph arrangement.
