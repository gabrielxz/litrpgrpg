## Master prompt

Redraw this provided angular cyan reference as the precise segmented clave, the System's mark in Gradebreaker. This is a refinement of Gabriel's personal drawing, NOT a new symbol design. Use the reference ONLY for the clave's exact silhouette, orientation, proportions and topology; its glow and photographic surface are forbidden in this version.

OUTPUT: a single square image, 2048 by 2048 pixels or larger, with one centered mark filling about 60 percent of the canvas width. Keep generous negative space. Solid flat electric cyan #42E9F5 on completely uniform deep blue-black #101B22. Both colors flat and uniform, no illumination gradients. Sharp clean graphic silhouette, vector-like edges, no other marks.

PRESERVE EXACTLY: One long diagonal stroke rising from lower left to upper right. At the lower-left end it turns down and right into a large OPEN angular hook; at the upper-right end it turns upward and left into the corresponding OPEN angular hook. The two hooks are opposing ends of one elongated infinity-like gesture, NEVER two closed loops or chain links. The reference's substantial open mouths and internal negative spaces remain. A SHORT VERTICAL BAR crosses the diagonal at its midpoint; protect its orientation, its visibility above and below the diagonal, and its relative length. The overall width-to-height ratio, angle, bar placement and lobe proportions should match the reference. Do not rotate, mirror or widen into a horizontal infinity sign.

REFINEMENT: Every outline and inner outline consists only of straight segments with deliberate 45-degree or chamfered corners; no curves or rounded terminals. Compose the existing clave from a SMALL NUMBER of smaller clean angular glyph pieces, arranged along its original stroke. Think six to nine substantial invented-script shapes fitting together with a few tiny clean hairline breaks. Vary the pieces subtly: an open angle, a hooked bracket, a stepped section, an elongated diagonal and a recognizable upright central stroke. They together preserve the outer contour as one instantly recognizable clave. Keep segmentation sparse and controlled, with no new branches that change the silhouette. The diagonal must still read as a long continuous gesture and the center must read as an upright crossing, not a fragmented intersection. Make each piece a substantial legible shape rather than decorating the mark with microscopic cuts. Protect the two hook openings above all. At only 8 mm wide it must remain recognizable.

The art direction is disciplined System geometry, a mark of observation: precise invented angular script with controlled open forms. In this asset the ordinary gouache/cut-paper world is absent; this is the System's clean functional mark. No extra ticks are necessary. No glow, halo, bleed, blur, gradient, bevel, extrusion, chrome, shading, texture, particles, reflections, lighting effects, text, numbers, other symbols, frame or ring. Do not insert the game's compass ring. No jagged lightning bolt or circuit-board branches. Do not present variations or a contact sheet: just the single refined clave.
