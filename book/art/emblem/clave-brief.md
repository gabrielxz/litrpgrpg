# The clave: the System's mark

The brief for regenerating the System's symbol. ChatGPT generates from it under the art bible's project instructions. The reference is the angular cyan variant from Gabriel's Codex explorations (`system-clavered-cyan-angular-v1.png`); the original is Gabriel's own drawing of the symbol, about twenty years old.

## What the mark is

The clave is the small mark that sat in the corner of the message every human heard on Day 1 and sits in the corner of every System notice since. The System has never named it or explained it. Humans call it the clave. It is the System's mark and the game's emblem at once (ruled 2026-09-23; the compass ring is retired). It appears on the chapter rules, the System message box, the quest card, the kit's masthead, the companion app's icon, and the cover.

## The shape, which does not change

- One elongated stroke running diagonally from lower left to upper right, shaped like an infinity sign pulled long.
- Two lobes, one at each end, both open: each lobe is a hook, never a closed loop.
- One short vertical stroke crossing the center of the diagonal. This bar is what keeps the mark from reading as an infinity sign, an S, or chain links. Protect it in every variant.
- The same orientation and proportions as the reference. Generous negative space around the mark.

## The direction for the rework

- More angular than the reference. Every edge straight, every corner a deliberate 45-degree or chamfered cut, inner and outer edges alike. No curves, no rounded terminals.
- The mark is built out of smaller pieces: read as a glyph string or cluster in the art bible's vocabulary (varied angular characters arranged into strings or clusters) that together form the clave. Each piece is a legible small shape; the whole reads as the clave at a glance from across a table. The pieces may be separated by hairline gaps or joined; the silhouette stays continuous enough that the mark is unmistakable at 8 millimeters.
- Flat, solid, luminous cyan on the art bible's blue-black. No halo, no glow bleed, no gradient, no bevel, extrusion, chrome, texture, particles, or reflections.
- The art bible's intent grammar applies: observation is fine open brackets and radial ticks; recognition is fragments resolving into alignment. The clave is the mark of observation. If any secondary detail is added, it is one or two fine ticks, never a frame or a ring.
- No readable text, numbers, or other symbols in the image.

## Deliverables

1. The mark on blue-black, square, 2048 by 2048 or larger, centered, the mark filling about 60 percent of the width.
2. The same mark as a flat single-color shape on a transparent background, for the kit and the pipeline (the pipeline keys cyan to transparent the way the compass ring was keyed).
3. The same mark in ink on bone ivory, for print surfaces where cyan cannot emit light.
4. A contact sheet showing the mark at three sizes (full, 25 millimeters, 8 millimeters) so legibility at the small size can be judged before anything is placed.
